# Architecture (salon-mobile, run-2, scoped quick run)

1. **Product and principals.** Same app and source as run 1 (salon-mobile/security/run-1/architecture.md): one-salon Expo/React Native app, no server, all data in AsyncStorage on the device. Principals: **owner** (owner password), **stylists/staff** (mobile + password; a shared salon phone or their own phone, a deployment fact), **customers**, the **supplier of a restored backup**, and for the legal pack the **person who runs the build script** and edits `src/config/salon-details.json`. The role comes only from the session (App.tsx:110-120); engine functions take no role and accept caller-supplied `staffId`/`by` values.
2. **Comparable baseline.** Server-backed salon software with per-user audit trails (run-1 baseline); here, audit fields are written by the same device and clock that staff hold. Calibration only.
3. **Scope.** Only the five units run 1 deferred: (A) attendance check-in and leave, (B) appointments, (C) customer privacy lifecycle, (D) the owner's end-of-day cash count, (E) scripts/build-legal.mjs. Every other unit is out of scope here and was reviewed in run 1 at the same source; run-1 findings (demo-data fallback, unvalidated backup restore, staff undo with clock set back, wallet-close overpayment; WhatsApp-code and photoKey needs-validation) stand and are not re-verified.
4. **Entry surfaces to sinks.**
   - (A) Attendance.tsx selfie `shot.at` (device time at capture) → `markAttendance` (engine.ts:1331; clock guard at :1336 against `latestRecordTime`, which ignores appointments, leaves, reminders, dayClosings and privacyLog, engine.ts:61-72) → attendance record; late flag recomputed (rules.ts:110, engine.ts:1361). Me.tsx → `requestLeave`/`withdrawLeave` (:1443, :1485; withdraw checks caller-supplied staffId). LeaveRequests.tsx (pushed from owner screens; no role check of its own) → `approveLeave`/`declineLeave` (:1520, :1555; no actor) → reassigns or cancels other customers' bookings and returns WhatsApp texts with customer names and phones.
   - (B) BookSheet.tsx → `bookAppointment` (:1022; consent, past-time by device clock, slot, leave); Calendar.tsx and TodayBookings.tsx → `cancelAppointment` (:1073, status check only, no actor), `markNoShow` (:1079, time-gated by device clock), `appointmentArrived` (:1087 → startVisit; TodayBookings falls back to `data.staff[0].id`), `markAppointmentReminded` (:1185, no status check). TodayBookings lists every booking to every staff member.
   - (C) Profile.tsx → `setMarketingConsent` (:806, any session, caller `by`), `customerDataExport` (:873, any session, logs DATA_SHARED with `by: null`, opened in WhatsApp), owner-gated `deleteCustomer`/`erase` (:919/:900; wallet must be 0; scrubs name, phone, referral code, removes reminders and appointments; visits, walletTx notes, referral links and privacyLog remain). `purgeExpired` (:935) on every start (store.ts:38) with cutoffs from `new Date()` and no clock guard; retention 1095/365/365 days (salon-details.json). Reminders: `logReminder` requires marketing consent (:747); `logReminderCall` limits to the last stylist only when `by` is non-null (:785).
   - (D) owner Live/Guard.tsx → `closeDay` (checks.ts:112; whole-rupee count, note on mismatch, re-count overwrites the day's record at :120, no actor) and `expectedCash` (:103; PAID cash bills by `paidAt` + TOPUP with note exactly "Paid by CASH" by `createdAt`; CASHOUT refunds and wallet+cash splits not considered) → `ownerChecks` (:127) cash over/short flag.
   - (E) `node scripts/build-legal.mjs [details.json] [--strict]` → `{{token}}` substitution from the details file into legal/*.md (unknown token throws) → minimal Markdown-to-HTML `inline()` with `esc` escaping only `& < >` (:78) and link regexes putting URLs into `href="…"` (:84-85) → `legal-out/<slug>/` (slug limited to `[a-z0-9-]`). Pages are published manually as static files; host and headers are deployment facts.
5. **Trust boundaries and strongest control.** Staff vs owner: UI gating only (leave approval, delete, cash count); staff vs staff: caller-supplied staffId (withdrawLeave is the only engine actor check); staff vs owner checks: device-clock timestamps with a guard only on check-in; customer PII lifecycle: erase/purge scrub the customer row but not every copy; legal build: build-time operator input.
6. **Starting paths.** src/domain/engine.ts, src/domain/checks.ts, src/domain/rules.ts, src/domain/store.ts, src/screens/{Attendance,Me,LeaveRequests,Calendar,TodayBookings,Profile,Reminders}.tsx, src/components/BookSheet.tsx, src/screens/owner/{Guard,Live,Staff}.tsx, scripts/build-legal.mjs, src/config/salon-details.json, legal/, tests/{leave,appointments,privacy,checks,engine}.test.ts.
7. **Prior coverage.** Run-1 ledger and findings read (same source). The five deferred units become current planned work (`prior_deferred`); the twelve run-1 units are recorded out of scope for this scoped run.
8. **Companion selection.** DESKTOP-MOBILE-AND-LOCAL-IPC.md for (A) (device-clock and pending-action trust on a shared phone); DATA-ISOLATION-AND-LIFECYCLE.md for (C) (erasure, retention purge, derived copies); CLIENT-SIDE.md for (E) (generated HTML rendered in a browser). (B) and (D) use ordinary classes only. Other companions excluded as in run 1 (no HTTP server, RPC, cloud, CI, native code, model, or shared service).
