A poster you can work from

Takes the vermilion half of your reference and makes it the whole screen: the salon name stacked huge like a shop poster, a round stamp in the corner, and a cream sheet holding the button and today's numbers as a clean list.

Motions used: #9 letter-by-letter headline and #8 grid pop-in on open, R6 press squeeze on every button, #12 shared-element morph when a tile opens, R1 materialize for the opened screen's content. With your phone's reduce-motion setting on, everything appears without animation.
