Inside the app

Staff and owner screens

Everything continues the approved login: the brown studio backdrop, frosted-glass cards, gold for money and the main action, and Urbanist type. Staff and owner see different apps after login. Staff get a dock for the work they do all day; the owner gets a dock for money, attendance, staff and visits.

Readable in a bright salon

Text is near-white on deep brown and every number is large. Glass cards are tinted enough that text never sits on a busy background.

One gold thing per screen

Gold marks money and the main action only: wallet seals, "Collect ₹600", the PAID stamp. Everything else is cream on brown.

Thumb-first

The main action sits low on the screen. The dock's round gold button always means "customer walked in".

Motion from the approved list

Card morphs, letter reveal, grid pop-in, list stagger, sliding tabs, receipt print, barber-pole progress. The keypad never animates.
