Inside the app

Staff and owner screens

Everything continues the approved login: the brown studio backdrop, frosted-glass cards, gold for money and the main action, and Urbanist type. Staff and owner see different apps after login. Staff get a dock for the work they do all day; the owner gets a dock for money, attendance, staff and visits.

Readable in a bright salon

Text is near-white on deep brown and every number is large. Glass cards are tinted enough that text never sits on a busy background.

One gold thing per screen

Gold marks money and the main action only: wallet seals, "Collect ₹600", the PAID stamp. Everything else is cream on brown.

Thumb-first

The main action sits low on the screen. The dock's round gold button always means "customer walked in".

Motion, now with P1–P6

Approved list plus the new ones: a button that becomes a ✓ (P1), stretchy tabs (P2), charts that draw while numbers settle (P3), an export status pill (P4), the gold flood into PAID (P5), staggered stops (P6). Gold “Tap” hints show what to try. The keypad never animates.

Owner's phone

Owner installs the app and signs in with the owner password. Sees everything live from anywhere: the floor, earnings, attendance selfies, staff, visits.

Each stylist's phone

Every staff member installs the same app and signs in with their own mobile number. They check in, serve their chair and see only their own earnings.

Synced, private, backed up

Every phone syncs to the salon's own private online store, so data is never lost with a phone. Works offline and catches up when the internet returns.

One salon, one app

Each salon gets its own app with its name, logo and prices. No salon can see another salon's data.
