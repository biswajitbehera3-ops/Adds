The front page

Wall on top, counter below

The top half carries the salon's identity: the razor, ESTD year and wordmark, which change per salon. The vermilion half is the working area, with the one big “Next customer” button and today's numbers as stamped tiles.

  • Placeholder name and year. Each salon gets its own wordmark and year from its config.
  • Fonts: Archivo (wide, stamped capitals) plus Yellowtail script for the greeting only.
  • The razor is drawn in code here. For the final app we can use a real photographed razor like your reference.
  • Plays motion 01 Badge & razor intro on load.
