Owner earnings: pick a layout

Each layout shows every stylist's customers and earnings, compares Day, Week and Month with the previous period, and opens a stylist's customers and services when tapped. The left phone works: switch the period and tap a stylist. The right phone shows what opens. Sample data: Wednesday 14 Oct, 4:10 PM.

Layout 1

Ledger with open-in-place rows

Period switch at the top, the total with "this vs previous" bars, then one row per stylist. Tapping a row opens it right there to list their customers, services and amounts, so the owner never leaves the screen.

Layout 2

Trend chart, then a full stylist page

Period switch, the total, and a bar chart of the last 7 days, 8 weeks or 6 months with this period in gold and the previous one marked. Stylists are ranked below; tapping one opens a full page with their comparison and every customer.

Layout 3

Three comparison cards and stylist tiles

Today, 7 days and the month are always visible side by side, each with its change and a small trend line; tap a card to switch. Stylists are tiles; tapping one slides up a sheet with their customers and services.
