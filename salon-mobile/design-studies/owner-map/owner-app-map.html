Owner app: what each tab opens

The owner signs in with the owner password and gets this bottom bar. Each tab opens one screen; Earnings has more inside it. Screens are from the approved designs with example data (Wednesday 14 Oct).

Live Earnings Attendance Staff Visits

1. The five tabs

Tap a tab in the bottom bar and this screen opens.

1Live

Live floor

The animated salon, live. Each chair shows the customer, service, stylist and minutes left. Customers walk in when a visit starts and walk out when it's paid. Front desk line and today's total.

2Earnings

Earnings

Today / 7 days / Month cards, the total against the previous period, the trend chart and every stylist's customers and earnings. More in section 2.

3Attendance

Attendance

Today's check-in selfies (green on time, orange late), the month grid per stylist, and Export to Excel.

4Staff

Staff

Every stylist with number and chair; edit, reset password, deactivate; add a new staff member.

5Visits

Visits

Every bill for today, 7 days or 30 days. Void a wrong bill with a reason (refunds the wallet, takes back a referral).

2. Inside Earnings

Tap one of the three cards at the top to switch the whole screen; tap a stylist to open their page.

Tap a card: Today, 7 days or Month
The total, the comparison and the chart change with the card. Stylists re-rank for that period.
AToday card

Today so far vs last Wednesday by this time; bars for the last 7 days.

B7 days card

Last 7 days vs the 7 days before; bars for the last 8 weeks.

CMonth card

October so far vs September 1–14; bars for the last 6 months.

Tap a stylist → their page
Their total and change, a line graph that follows the Day / Week / Month switch on their page, and their customers. "‹ Earnings" goes back.
DDay

Each day of the last 7 days (Tuesday closed, today so far). Below: today's customers with time, service, amount and payment.

EWeek

Each week of October, September dashed behind; badge "vs September" (8–14 Oct vs 8–14 Sep). Below: day-by-day earnings.

FMonth

The last 12 months, best month labelled, October so far. Below: week-by-week and top services.
