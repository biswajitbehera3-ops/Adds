Staff Calendar: three animated setups

Each phone below is live; tap days, swipe, flip My bookings / Whole salon, tap a booking and press Mark arrived (it updates all three phones). Example data for Imran, Wednesday 14 Oct 2026, 2:10 PM; Tuesdays closed.

Recommended: B, week strip + day timeline. A stylist mostly asks "what's left today, and when is my next gap", which a timeline answers at a glance; the month is one tap away on "October ▾". In Whole salon it turns into one column per stylist, so they can see who is free for a walk-in. A is the month view you approved, now animated; C suits a stylist who only wants a running list.

Motion spec (goes into the build)

From the design-motion-principles skill, weighted for a mobile app used all day: Jakub Krehel's subtle polish first, Emil Kowalski's restraint second. Nothing loops or pulses, only transform / opacity / blur / clip-path animate, and with the phone's "reduce motion" setting everything switches instantly.
