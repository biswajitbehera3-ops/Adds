Staff Calendar: month first, then the day

The Calendar tab opens on the month. Tap a day and the month folds down into that week's strip, and the day opens as a timeline (setup B). "‹ October" unfolds the month again. Example data for Imran, Wednesday 14 Oct 2026, 2:10 PM; Tuesdays closed.

1Opens on the month

Every day with bookings gets a glow and a gold ring that fills to how booked it is, with the booking count in the corner; nearly full days (75%+) turn solid gold. When the month opens, the rings sweep in once, rippling out from today. Tuesdays are faded. Below, the Today card shows who's next and how full the day is. ‹ › changes month.

2Tap a day

The gold highlight moves to the day. The other weeks fade out and the month folds up into a single row: that week. The day's timeline fades in under it. Tapping the Today card does the same for today.

3The day (setup B)

Hours down the side, bookings as blocks sized to their length, a gold line at the current time. Tap another day in the strip; swipe the strip for the next week, or the timeline for the next day.

4Whole salon

The same switch as before. In the day view it shows one column per stylist, so they can see who is free for a walk-in. On the month it changes the counts.

5Tap a booking

A card rises from the bottom: time, bill, visit number, Call, WhatsApp, and on the day, Mark arrived · start visit. Flick down to close.

6Back to the month

"‹ October 2026" at the top unfolds the month again, with the day you were on still highlighted.

7+ Book

One sheet, five parts that unlock in order: customer (10-digit mobile; a returning customer shows visits, last service and wallet, a new one needs a name and a reminders yes/no), services (time and price add up), stylist (you by default; anyone who can't do those services is greyed; or "Any free"), day and time (next 14 days, Tuesdays off; only times where the whole service fits are open), and an optional note with the WhatsApp confirmation preview. Book → the day opens and the new booking glows into place. You can also tap an empty spot on your own timeline to book that time.
