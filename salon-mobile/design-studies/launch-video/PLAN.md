# Launch video: director's plan (v2, 28 Oct 2026)

**Status:** planned, not built. Build only when the user says so (BUILD-SPEC 11.1).
**Brief (user, 28 Oct):** a proper launch video that tells a story, so an owner understands exactly what happens after they buy the app. Keep the user's angle: customers who never come back are brought back by reminders and referral credits; the owner sees the salon live, the day's earnings, each stylist's earnings, attendance and leave.

## The idea

**Title: "Your first 30 days."**

The film follows one salon from the day the app arrives to the day a lost customer walks back in. The viewer is the owner, so the film speaks to them: *your* salon, *your* stylists. No actor plays the owner. The phone screen is the main character, and the captions carry the story like a silent film.

The film has three time stamps, each a promise of what happens after buying:
- **Day 1:** we set it up for you.
- **Every day:** you see everything.
- **Day 30:** they come back, and they bring friends.

Every shot is a real screen from the app, with an invented salon's data. Nothing shown is a mock-up, and no feature is shown that the app can't do today.

## Format
- **Length:** 60 seconds (the full story), plus a 20-second cut of Act 3 alone for WhatsApp status and Reels.
- **Frame:** vertical 1080×1920, for WhatsApp and Instagram.
- **Look:** dark brown and gold like the app, Urbanist font, the phone in the centre with soft depth, gold captions above it.
- **Sound:** music, no voice. Where a payment happens, the app's own payment chime plays (`SOUNDS.md`).
- **Demo salon:** an invented salon called **"Your Salon"**, so the owner pictures their own. A per-prospect version with the prospect's own salon name and prices can be made for each pitch (it is code, so it costs nothing to re-render).

## Director's rules for the edit
1. **Pain first, product second.** The first 5 seconds show no app.
2. **One idea per shot.** Every cut adds one new fact. If a shot repeats one, it is cut.
3. **Follow the money and the customer.** One customer, **Priya**, is followed from first visit to coming back with a friend, so the story has a thread.
4. **Cut on the action.** Every cut lands on a tap, a sum changing or a chair filling, and on the music's beat.
5. **Hold the payoff.** The three "result" moments (the chair fills, the day's total, Priya returns) hold a beat longer than the rest.
6. **Plain words.** Captions of 8 words or fewer, a 5th-grade reading level, one line where possible.
7. **Calm movement.** Smooth slides and gentle push-ins only: no bounce, no shake, no flash.

## What each promise in the film rests on (honesty check)
| Promise | Where it is real today |
|---|---|

| Start a walk-in in seconds | Counter keypad → new customer → Start visit (timed in `SALON-DAY-TEST.md`) |

| WhatsApp thank-you and reminders | One tap by staff (`PLAN-24-OCT.md`: never automatic) |
| Earnings per day and per stylist | Earnings tab, stylist earnings |
| Leave requests | Leave requests screen, owner approves |
| Undone bills and cash checks | Today's checks |
| Due-back list | Reminders screen, from each service's due-back days |
| ₹100 referral credit each | `src/config/salon.ts` referral rewards (per salon, can change) |

The film makes **no number claims** about results (no "30% more customers"): there are no real salon results yet.

## How it gets built
1. Write a recording script that drives the current build (the one that passed security check run 3) in a phone-sized browser. It sets up "Your Salon" from empty through the real screens, so the Day 1 shots are real. It then plays out a day with Priya, the other walk-ins and the check-ins, then moves the clock 30 days ahead for Act 3.
2. Record each shot as video at high resolution, and trim on the action.
3. Lay out the frame, phone, captions and title cards in code, cut to the music's beat, and render the MP4 with ffmpeg.
4. Music: a licensed track from the connected Epidemic Sound account, chosen for the soft-open → lift → warm-peak shape above, cut to 60 and 20 seconds. Listen to it before using it (earshot).
5. Watch every frame and listen to the full mix, fix anything wrong, then show it. Save it here and mark BUILD-SPEC 11.1 done.
