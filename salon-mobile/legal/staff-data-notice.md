# Staff Data Notice — [Salon name]

Given to every staff member when their login is created.

**What we keep about you:** your name, mobile number (your login), chair, password (stored only as a scrambled hash; nobody can read it), daily check-in time, your attendance selfie, and the customers and amounts you served.

**Why:** to run attendance and pay, to show you your own earnings, and to show the owner who served whom. This is processing for employment purposes, which the DPDP Act allows (section 7(i)); we still tell you about it here.

**Who sees it:** the owner sees everyone's attendance and earnings. You see only your own earnings and customers. The app provider stores it securely in India for the salon.

**How long:**
- selfies: [12 months], then deleted automatically, keeping only the check-in time
- attendance and earnings records: as long as needed for wages and tax records
- when you leave: your login is turned off at once, and your personal details are deleted [12 months] later, apart from what tax and labour law require us to keep

**Your rights:** ask the owner for a copy, a correction, or deletion (subject to what law requires us to keep). Contact [owner name, phone]. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

**The selfie is not face recognition.** It is only a photo the owner can look at.
