## 2. What we collect, and why
We collect only what we need.

| Data | Why we need it | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Name and mobile number | To find your record at the counter, run your visits and wallet, send your bill | Your consent |
| Services taken, stylist, date, amount, payment method | To bill you, keep your visit history, remind you when a service is due (if you opt in), meet tax law | Your consent; legal obligation for bills |
| Wallet top-ups, bonus, spends, refunds | To hold and use your prepaid wallet money | Your consent |
| Appointments you book: day, time, services, stylist, and your reply if you booked on WhatsApp | To keep your slot, send a booking confirmation and an appointment reminder on the day | Your consent (you asked for the booking) |
| Who referred you / whom you referred | To credit referral rewards | Your consent |
| Whether you are under 18 (record kept under a parent's/guardian's number) | To get a parent's or guardian's consent, as the law requires | Legal obligation |
| A record of your consent (when, notice version, staff who took it) | To prove we asked you properly | Legal obligation |

**We do not collect:** your address, email, date of birth, photos of you, ID documents, location, or contacts. The app has **no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies**.

## 3. WhatsApp reminders and offers (optional)
We send them only if you tick the separate box for them. Each one says how to stop. Reply **STOP**, or ask at the counter, and they stop. Saying no does not affect our service to you. Your bill can still be sent on WhatsApp, without offers.

Appointment confirmations and on-the-day reminders are part of a booking you asked for, so they're sent even if you have not opted in to offers. Reply **C** to cancel a booking.

## 4. Children
For anyone under 18 we keep the record under a parent's or guardian's mobile number, only with that adult's consent given in person. We never send offers to a child's own number.

## 5. Who can see your data
- Our staff, to look you up and serve you at the counter. Each staff member has their own login.
- The owner.
- The provider, under contract, only to run and support the app. When online backup and sync are switched on, its hosting company ({{cloudProvider}}, servers in {{cloudRegion}}) stores the data too.
- WhatsApp (Meta) carries a message when we send one to you.
- Government authorities, only when the law requires.
We **never sell** your data and never share it for anyone else's marketing.

## 7. Your rights
You can, free of charge:
1. **Get a copy** of your data. We can send it to you on WhatsApp.
2. **Correct** or complete it.
3. **Withdraw consent**: stop messages, or withdraw entirely, which deletes your record.
4. **Delete** your data. We first refund your unused paid wallet money (see the Refund Policy).
5. **Nominate** someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.
6. **Complain** to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use them, ask at the counter or contact our grievance officer below. We reply within **7 days**, and in any case within the time the law allows.

## 8. Security
Data is kept in the app's private storage on the salon's phones. When online backup and sync are on, it is also stored encrypted, in India, and separated so no other salon can see it. Each staff member has their own login, and passwords are stored only as scrambled hashes. Selfies and old data are deleted on schedule.
If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board without delay, as the DPDP Rules require.
