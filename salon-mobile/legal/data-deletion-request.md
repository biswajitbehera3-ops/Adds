# Data Deletion and Other Privacy Requests

## How a customer can ask
- **At the counter** (fastest): staff open the customer's profile.
- **WhatsApp or phone** to the grievance officer: {{ownerName}}, {{ownerPhone}}. Email: {{ownerEmail}}.
- **Reply STOP** to any reminder to stop messages only.

## What staff and the owner do (all in the app)
| Request | Who | Where in the app | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stop messages / STOP reply | Any staff | Profile → Privacy → untick "WhatsApp reminders and offers" | Same day |
| Copy of my data | Any staff | Profile → Privacy → "Send them their data on WhatsApp" | Same day (law: without delay) |
| Correct my name or number | Owner | Profile | 7 days |
| Delete my data | Owner | Profile → Privacy → close wallet (refund) → "Delete customer data" (tap twice) | 7 days |
| Complaint | Owner | Reply personally; note the outcome | 7 days |

**Check identity first:** the request must come from the customer's own number, or the customer in person.
For a child's record, it must come from the parent or guardian whose number is on file.

## What deletion does
- Erases the name, mobile number, referral code and consent, and deletes saved copies of messages.
- Keeps the bills (amounts, dates, services) **without** name or number, because tax law requires the salon to keep bills.
- Writes a log entry with only the date and who did it, as proof the request was handled.
- The person can come back later as a new customer.
