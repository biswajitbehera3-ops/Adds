# Refund Policy

Two parts: **A** is for salon customers (shown at the counter and in the app). **B** is for salons paying for the app.

## A. Customers of [Salon name]

### Services
If you are not happy with a service, tell us before you leave. We will redo it or adjust the bill. A bill entered by mistake is cancelled (voided) and any wallet money used goes back to your wallet.

### Wallet money
| Money in your wallet | Refundable? |
|---|---|
| Money you paid in (top-ups) | **Yes**: any unused amount, whenever you ask to close your wallet |
| Bonus on a top-up (for example 10% extra) | No. It is a free extra |
| Referral rewards | No. They are a free extra |

- Bonus and referral credit are **used first** when you pay, so the money you paid in stays refundable as long as possible.
- Refunds are paid by cash, UPI or card within **7 days**, by the method you choose.
- Wallet money does not expire while the salon is open. If the salon closes for good, unused paid-in money is refunded.
- Deleting your data closes your wallet: we refund the paid-in balance first.
- A referral reward is taken back if the visit that earned it is cancelled.

The app works this out automatically and shows the refundable amount before anything is paid out.

## B. Salons paying for the app
| Payment | Refund |
|---|---|
| Setup fee | Full refund if the Provider doesn't deliver a working app within [14] days of the agreed date. No refund after the app is delivered and accepted |
| Monthly fee | Paid in advance. Cancelling stops the next month; the current month isn't refunded part-way |
| Outage | If sync or storage is unavailable for more than [24] hours in a month through the Provider's fault, that month is credited [pro rata] |
| Overcharge or duplicate payment | Refunded in full within 7 days |

Refunds go back by the original payment method. GST is adjusted with a credit note.

*Before go-live, ask your CA how GST applies to wallet top-ups (advance for services). Ask a lawyer to confirm the wallet terms.*
