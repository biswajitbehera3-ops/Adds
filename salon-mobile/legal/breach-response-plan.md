# Personal Data Breach Response Plan (internal)

A breach is any unauthorised access, loss, change or disclosure of personal data. Examples: a lost or stolen staff phone that is still logged in, a leaked password, a cloud misconfiguration, or a wrong export sent to someone.

| Step | When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Contain | Immediately | Owner / Provider | Deactivate the affected logins (Owner → Staff); change the owner password; Provider revokes that device's sync access |
| 2. Tell the Provider / the Salon | Within 24 hours | Whoever finds it | WhatsApp + email with what happened, when, what data, how many people |
| 3. Assess | Within 48 hours | Provider + owner | What data, whose, risk of harm; keep notes |
| 4. Inform each affected person | Without delay | Owner (template below) | What happened, likely consequences, what we did, what they can do, contact |
| 5. Inform the Data Protection Board | Intimation without delay; **detailed report within 72 hours** (DPDP Rules, Rule 7) | Owner, helped by the Provider | Through the Board's online portal |
| 6. Fix and learn | Within 7 days | Provider | Root cause, fix, update this plan |

**Message template to customers:**
> {{salonName}}: On ‹date› we found that ‹what happened›. This may have exposed your ‹name and mobile number / visit history›. We have ‹action taken›. Please ‹be careful of calls or messages claiming to be from us›. Questions: {{ownerName}}, {{ownerPhone}}.

Keep a log of every breach, even small ones, for at least 1 year.
