# Data Processing Agreement (DPDP Act, 2023 — section 8(2))

**Data Fiduciary:** [salon legal name] ("the Salon"). **Data Processor:** [your business name] ("the Provider").
This agreement forms part of the Terms of Service.

1. **Instructions only.** The Provider processes the Salon's personal data only to provide the app (storage, sync, backups, support) and only on the Salon's documented instructions. It does not use the data for its own purposes, or for marketing, analytics or AI training.
2. **Data and people covered.** Customer name, mobile number, visits, payments, wallet and referrals; staff name, mobile number, login secret (hashed), chair, attendance times and selfies; consent and privacy-request records.
3. **Confidentiality.** Only named Provider staff with a need may access the data. They are bound to confidentiality, and access is logged.
4. **Security** (DPDP Rules, Rule 6): encryption in transit and at rest; each salon's data kept separate; per-person logins, with passwords stored only as salted hashes; access logs kept at least 1 year; daily backups; prompt security updates.
5. **Location.** Data is stored in India ([cloud provider], [region, e.g. Mumbai]).
6. **Sub-processors.** Current: [cloud provider] (hosting and database). The Provider gives the Salon 30 days' notice of any new sub-processor; the Salon may object and cancel.
7. **Breach.** The Provider tells the Salon without undue delay, and in any case within **24 hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with the facts known. It helps the Salon inform the Data Protection Board and the affected people (Board report within 72 hours).
8. **Help with rights.** The app lets the Salon export, correct, stop messages for, and delete a customer. The Provider helps with anything the app cannot do, within 7 days.
9. **Retention and return.** The Provider follows the Salon's retention settings. At the end of the service it returns all data (Excel export) and deletes it, including backups, within 30 days, then confirms in writing.
10. **Audit.** Once a year, or after a breach, the Salon may ask for a written description of the security measures and access logs for its data.
11. **Liability** as in the Terms of Service, except that the cap does not apply to breaches of this agreement caused by the Provider's negligence.
