# Cookie Policy

**The Salon app uses no cookies and no similar tracking.**
It has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media SDKs (see `third-party-audit.md`).
The app keeps only what it needs to work on the phone: your login session and the salon's data (in app storage, not cookies).

**The privacy and terms web pages** (the links in the app) are plain static pages with **no cookies, no analytics, and no embedded third-party content**. Under DPDP and international practice, a consent banner is required only for non-essential cookies, so none is shown.

**If analytics or any non-essential cookie is ever added** to those pages:
1. Turn on the ready-made banner in `cookie-banner.html`. Nothing loads until the visitor clicks **Accept**. **Reject** is equally easy, and the choice is remembered.
2. Update this policy with each cookie's name, purpose, provider and lifetime.
3. Bump the policy date.
