# GTA 6 launch week: what Rockstar will probably publish + the guide/fact videos to have ready

Researched 15 Nov 2026 from Rockstar Newswire, the igta5.com GTA V launch timeline, Rockstar's X account and YouTube search data.

## 1. What Rockstar published last time (the pattern)

| Game | When | What Rockstar released | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA V | 16 Sep 2013 (day before launch) | Free **iFruit** companion app + the **full game manual** online | igta5.com launch timeline |
| GTA V | 17 Sep 2013 | Launch-day press release | Take-Two IR |
| GTA V | 1 Oct 2013 (2 weeks later) | GTA Online launched | igta5.com |

| RDR2 | 26 Oct 2018 | Official **Companion App** (iOS/Android); **Complete Official Guide** book (sold by a publisher, announced in July) | IGN, Newswire 23 Jul 2018 |
| RDR2 | **5 Nov 2018 (10 days after launch)** | Newswire **"Red Dead Redemption 2 Quick Tips"**: 9 tips (lost hat, faster looting, quick map, horse melee, cinematic hands-free travel, warning shot…) | Rockstar Newswire |
| RDR2 | 5 Nov 2018 | Rockstar's X post sent fans to the tips **and to community-made guides by named creators/sites** | x.com/RockstarGames |

**Pattern:** Rockstar does **not** post mission walkthroughs. It posts (a) a "get ready / pre-load" post before launch (done for GTA VI on 3 and 10 Nov), (b) sometimes a companion app or manual at launch, (c) a short **official tips post about 1–2 weeks after launch**, and (d) it boosts good community guides.

**GTA VI so far:** pre-load posts are out. **No companion app, digital manual or official guidebook announced** (checked 15 Nov). Already reported: boxed edition has no paper map, and a **free printable map on 19 Nov** (Forbes, REPORTED; confirm the day it drops).
