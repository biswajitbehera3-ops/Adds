# GTA 6: what we must NOT post (channel rule, from the user, 17 Nov 2026)

Breaking these gets videos taken down. They apply to every long video, Short, cover, clip and clipping campaign.

| # | Never post | Why | What we do instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leaked footage of any kind**, including **early unboxing videos** before launch | Taken down | Official Rockstar trailers, screenshots and posts only. For box/code stories use text cards and our own graphics, never someone's unboxing clip. |
| 2 | **The ending or any big story reveal** (anything about the storyline) | Taken down | Spoiler-free titles and covers. Talk about systems (police, guns, body, money), not plot. A SPOILER card + warning for anything story-related after 19 Nov, and nothing past the opening hours. |
| 3 | **Cutscenes posted on their own** | Taken down (fine only inside a longer playthrough) | Clips must be gameplay or a streamer's reaction to gameplay, not a cutscene with nothing else. |
| 4 | **In-game TV shows and comedy posted alone** (e.g. Macca & Friends on Weazel) | Taken down | We can *mention* them; we don't post their footage as a clip. |

## The money part people skip
Rockstar's permission only covers **fan footage that you don't make money from**. **Paid clipping sits outside it.**
- A clipping campaign paying you (Content Rewards, clipping.net, Kick Clipping, a streamer) is **not** permission from Rockstar.
- So every paid clip carries takedown and strike risk, even if the streamer allowed it.
- Checking campaigns and reading briefs is free and fine; decide per campaign whether the risk is worth it.
- Re-read Rockstar's own video/fan-content policy before monetising anything.
