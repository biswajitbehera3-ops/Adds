# voice.md — GTA 6 English channel

Read by every `yt-*` skill. Built from `production/style-guide.md`. **Durable copy lives here in the repo**; copy it to
`~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` at session start (the cloud container resets).

## Who I am talking to

One person: a GTA fan, 16–30, who has watched every trailer more than once and is tired of videos that pad ten minutes out of
two facts and call a Reddit rumour "confirmed". They want to know exactly what's true and what to do about it before
19 November. Many don't own a current-gen console yet. English-speaking, worldwide, a big share in India.

## How I actually talk

_No recorded videos yet — replace this with the transcript of the first three uploads once they exist._
Until then, the target is the spoken lines in `scripts/001`–`005`:
- Short sentences. Contractions. One idea per sentence.
- Says where every fact came from, out loud: "Rockstar says…", "Xbox confirmed…", "Digital Foundry reports…".
- Admits mistakes on camera ("a lot of people, including me, thought…").
- Gives the viewer something to do ("check your free space now", "update it this week").

## Words I never use

"Let's get into it", "without further ado", "smash that like button", "game-changer", "insane" as filler, "dive in", "leaked"
as a selling point, "100% confirmed" (it's either CONFIRMED with a source or it isn't), any Hindi.

## Words I do use

"Here's the catch." · "Confirmed by…" · "My bet is…" (only for theories) · "So here's what to do." · "Stay for the last one."

## What I will not claim

- Anything not traceable to Tier 1 (Rockstar, PlayStation Blog, Xbox Wire) or Tier 2 (named press) — see
  `research/gta6-news-sources.md`.
- Leaks, datamined pre-load files, insider accounts, prices from Reddit.
- Numbers nobody published (e.g. pre-order totals).
- Story spoilers after 19 Nov without a warning.
- That I've played the game before I have.

## My format

- 5–7 minute long-form, 2 per day around launch week, uploaded ~6 PM and ~9 PM IST.
- Voiceover only (no face cam), over official Rockstar footage + our own Remotion motion graphics.
- Opens with a 3-part hook in ≤25 s: pattern break → stakes → payoff tease. No intro sequence.
- Numbered chapters with a progress bar, a re-hook every 60–90 s, strongest item last.
- On-screen CONFIRMED / REPORTED / THEORY tag on every fact. Sources linked in the description.
- No swearing. Licensed music only. Ends on a comment question + end screen to the next video.
