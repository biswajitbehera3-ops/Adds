# Studio master plate (locked)

`studio_master_v1.png` is the channel's studio room, chosen by the channel owner (option C).

- Made with: SDXL 1.0 base + madebyollin/sdxl-vae-fp16-fix (CreativeML OpenRAIL++-M, commercial use allowed), on Kaggle.
- Source: `podcast/kaggle/characters/make_characters.py`, job `studio`, seed 2, 1344×768, 32 steps, guidance 7.0.
- Re-running that job with the same seed and model versions reproduces it.

Seating plan: the Genius sits in the blue armchair (left of the table); the Questioner sits on the navy sofa with the yellow cushions (right).

Known limits of v1: one camera angle only; 1344×768 (needs upscaling for 1080p/4K); the items on the table are not clearly podcast microphones.
