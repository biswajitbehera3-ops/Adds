# Content Rewards (Whop) campaign research — 6 Nov 2026

**Source:** contentrewards.com/c/discover (86 live campaigns), campaign pages for the shortlist, and guides on payouts.
**Assumes:** the clipper is in India, so **Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts only** (TikTok is banned in India).

## How the money works

- **Earnings = views × rate, up to a per-post cap, and only while the budget lasts.** Past the cap, extra views on that post earn nothing.
- **Instagram usually pays less than TikTok** on the same campaign (for example, CoD pays $1.35 on IG and $1.85 on TikTok).
- **Payout timing:** views keep earning for 7 days after approval, then there's a 3-day hold, so money settles about 10 days after approval.
- **Bot checks:** every post gets a 0–100 bot score. High scores go to human review, and a credited payout can be reversed if fraud is confirmed.
- **India payouts:** payout methods depend on country. One report from India (Aug 2026) said adding a payout method failed until a fix the next day.
  **Add and test your payout method before you start clipping.**

Because of the caps, **many clips of 100K–400K views earn more than one huge viral clip**. Post often.

## Avoid

- **Gambling or casino:** Mark Loves Casinos, Coinpoker, Flip.gg. Promoting gambling is legally risky in India, and these can get accounts flagged.
- **Adult:** Sinparty ($5/1K but 412 clippers and adult content).
- **Crypto:** FOMO TOKEN2049. It's allowed, but crypto promotion in India is risky.
- **Country-rated campaigns** like World of Warcraft [UK] and [Germany] pay by the viewer's country, so Indian viewers earn very little.
- **Nearly finished campaigns:** Backyard Breaks (98%), WatchMeWin (97%), ROBTHEBANK (98%).
