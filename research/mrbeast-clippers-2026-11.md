## What the winners have in common

1. **Followers don't matter.** An 81K account got 5.7M views on one clip. TikTok judges each clip on how it performs.
2. **Non-English clips win big.** 3 of the top accounts post in Spanish or Portuguese, and the best single clip in the set (14.8M) is Spanish. Far fewer clippers compete in those languages.
3. **Two styles work:**
   - **Story clip:** an all-caps curiosity caption and a stakes-first moment ("$250,000", "identical twin").
   - **Emotional edit:** a slowed or phonk song, slow-motion "aura" moments, and 😭💔 captions. This style gets the most shares.
4. **Focus on the crew and contestants**, not only MrBeast: Nolan, Tareq, Juan, "MrLean". These are characters viewers already know.
5. **Feel-good moments travel:** kindness, building wells in Africa, a contestant winning.
6. **Series hooks:** "Part 1" in the caption brings viewers back for the next part.
7. **Disclose the sponsorship:** every paid clip uses `#vyro`, `#mrbeastpartner` or `#paidpartner`. Check the Vyro brief for the exact tag it requires.

## Rough earnings (only an estimate)

Treat these numbers as the upper limit, not what they actually earned.

**Takeaways:**
- Yes, motion graphics are allowed and used. The brief decides exactly what you may add, and clips that ignore it are rejected.
- MrBeast's source footage **already has** timers, money graphics and name tags. Don't cover them; add what's missing.
- What clippers add themselves: clean captions, a hook line, edit effects (warp, zoom, slow motion) and music for emotional edits.
- Vyro rejects clips for not following the brief, missing the ad disclosure, or "low quality and spammy content". Botted views can get an account banned permanently.
