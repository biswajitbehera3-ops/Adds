# GTA VI: The Festival — Miami, Sat 21 Nov 2026 (research 22 Nov, IST)

## CONFIRMED (Rockstar)
- **What:** "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival", a launch concert, presented by **Rockstar Games and PlayStation**, produced by the **III Points** team. Two stages. ([Newswire, 9 Nov](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o39o87a435a25a/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival) · [festival page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/gtavi-thefestival))
