# What's happening in Miami for GTA 6 — checked 12 Nov 2026

## GTA VI: The Festival — CONFIRMED (Rockstar Newswire, 9 Nov)
- **When:** Saturday **21 November 2026**, two days after launch.
- **Where:** **Mana Wynwood, 318 NW 23rd St., Wynwood, Miami** (same grounds as the III Points festival).
- **Who runs it:** Rockstar Games **in partnership with PlayStation**, produced by the creators of **III Points**. Two stages.
- **Lineup (Rockstar's exact list):** Cactus Jack presents **Travis Scott**, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, and Sofaygo; **Cardi B**;
  Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso; **Justice (DJ set)** — Justice composed the game's original score; **Metro Boomin**; Nick León B2B DJ
  Python; Prospa B2B Skream; Rauw & Friends; Suero; Tainy; The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set). Artists are from the game and its
  official soundtrack.
- **Tickets:** lottery only (closed 10 Nov, 4 PM ET); winners got SMS codes; sale on DICE 11 Nov, 12 PM ET; max 2 per code,
  non-transferable; listed at **$60** on DICE; **waitlist** open now (USA Today). **17+ only.** No on-site parking —
  rideshare drop-offs around the perimeter.
- Sources: https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o39o87a435a25a/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival ·
  USA Today https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/state/florida/miami/2026/11/11/gta-6-festival-miami-tickets/92722158007/ ·
  Forbes, Billboard, GameSpot, DJ Mag.

## "A Night in Vice City" — CONFIRMED (Rockstar YouTube Short, 9 Nov)
- Wed **18 Nov, 7 PM ET** (= **5:30 AM IST, 19 Nov**), on ESPN: the **Miami HEAT "trade Miami for Vice City"** when they host the
  Milwaukee Bucks — the eve of launch. Link: https://HEAT.com/ViceCity · video https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Rj2Jnbt-5p4
  (880K views).
- Also note: Metro (12 Nov) says the viral street clip "recreates a video the developers took while visiting Miami", while Kotaku
  says it recreates an OnlyInDade viral video. Sources disagree — say "a viral Miami street video", not who filmed it.

## The city itself — REPORTED
- **Miami-Dade County: $3.9 million GTA VI marketing package** (county press release): themed lighting at **Miami International
  Airport** and **PortMiami**, displays on pedestrian walkways, wraps on traffic-signal cabinets, ads across the transit system.
  Includes an agreement with Rockstar. (USA Today)
- **"Welcome to Vice City" sign on the roof of the Kaseya Center** (Miami Heat's arena) — a $975,000 deal, part of the $3.9M.
  (USA Today, Forbes)
- **Club Space "Vice City takeover", 20–22 Nov**, inspired by the in-game nightclub The Vault. (DJ Mag) — don't call it an
  official Rockstar event unless confirmed.
- **E11EVEN nightclub → "NINE1NINE"**, a GTA VI-inspired club, from 6 Nov. (DJ Mag) — same caution.
- **The viral "real Miami" clip:** Rockstar recreated an OnlyInDade street video in-game and posted it on Instagram (Kotaku,
  Polygon, IGN).
