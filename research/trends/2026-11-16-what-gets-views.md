## What's NOT working
- GTA Online weekly updates: TGG 162K–273K = **0.2–0.3×**.
- Plain weekly news recaps from small channels: JoblessGarrett "Everything GTA 6 This Week" 14K.
- The many copycat "10/12/13 things on Day 1" lists from small channels: 2K–10K each. The format works, but a copy with a weak title doesn't.
- Leak/datamine videos still get views (TGG "DATAMINED" 1.9×, "200+ hours" 82K), but we don't make them (fact rules).

## Shorts
- The "GTA 6 shorts" search is dominated by official trailers (PlayStation 6.5M, IGN 1.8M) and spam "GTA V live #shorts" streams.
- So there is no clear creator-Shorts winner to copy; it's open space for clean 30–60 s explainers.

## The one shared trait
The winners **talk to the viewer about the next 72 hours**: "before you play", "need to know", "do first", "finally downloading". Countdown urgency plus "you" beats plain news.
