## Launch timing: India unlocks before the US

- Store listings unlock GTA 6 at **midnight local time on 19 Nov, one time zone per country**. New Zealand is first.
- **India: 12:00 AM IST, 19 Nov** = 1:30 PM ET on 18 Nov. That's about **10.5 hours before the US** (midnight Eastern) and 5.5 hours
  before the UK.
- Rockstar hasn't announced a launch hour itself; these times come from store listings, so confirm on launch night.
- **Pre-load:** from 10 Nov (PS5 at midnight local time). The download needs up to 212 GB. The physical edition is a download code in a
  box, with no disc.
- **Your advantage:** Indian players can upload "first gameplay" before the US can play at all. Search demand is global and supply is
  low in those hours.

## Rockstar rules for long videos (official policy)

- ✅ **Full playthroughs and "Let's Play" with commentary**, including cutscenes **as part of a larger play-through**.
- ❌ **Isolated cutscenes, "all cutscenes = GTA 6 movie" videos, the ending on its own, edited combinations of story scenes.** These are
  taken down. They were a common trend at GTA 5's launch, so don't copy it.
- ❌ Anything before your country's unlock (no leaks, no "early unboxing" footage).
- ❌ Cheats, money glitches, mod menus.
- Put spoiler warnings in the title or thumbnail when needed, and don't put story spoilers in thumbnails.
- YouTube: violent gameplay in the first 7 seconds blocks ads, and rampages against civilians can get a video age-restricted.

## The plan

### Now to 18 Nov (no game needed)
Make **2–3 Hindi long videos** in the formats that are already working. You voice them and use short, credited trailer clips as part of
the commentary:
1. "GTA 6 आने से पहले सब कुछ जानो (Everything before you play)": release time in IST, pre-load, price in India, map, characters.
2. "50 Details You Missed in GTA 6 Trailers (Hindi)".
3. "GTA 6 India launch guide: unlock time, pre-load, 212 GB, price".
Trailer clips can still get Content ID claims, so keep your own commentary dominant.

### Launch night (19 Nov, 12:00 AM IST)
1. **Go live on YouTube at unlock**: "GTA 6 LIVE – पहला गेमप्ले (Hindi)". The live replay becomes your first long video.
2. Upload **"GTA 6 Gameplay Walkthrough Part 1 (Hindi)"** as fast as possible. You're ahead of the US for about 10 hours.
3. Cut **Shorts** from the stream for the clip page and for campaigns.

### Launch week onward
- **Daily series:** "Part 2, 3, 4…" or mission-by-mission (each mission title gets searched).
- Keep the **same thumbnail style and title pattern** every time, put every part in a playlist, add end screens linking to the next part,
  and add chapters.
- After the story: money guides ("How to make money fast in GTA 6"), challenges, map secrets.

### Title patterns that work (from the data)
- A number: "50 THINGS YOU MISSED…"
- Urgency: "IT'S FINALLY HERE…", "BREAKING:"
- Search terms first: "GTA 6 Gameplay Walkthrough Part 1"
- Hindi script works: Ujjwal's 12.77M title was in Hindi.
