Campaigns pay per view (uncertain). **Direct deals pay a fixed fee per clip or video**, so the money is predictable. Do both.

## Who to target

- **Mid-size streamers:** about 50–2,000 average live viewers, or 5K–200K followers. Big streamers already have teams; tiny ones can't pay.
- **They stream regularly** (GTA, CoD, Fortnite and so on), but their Shorts or Reels are **weak, irregular or missing**. That gap is your pitch.
- **Hindi and Indian gaming streamers:** easier to connect with, UPI payments, and GTA 6 in Hindi is a gap (see `gta6-youtube-longform-2026-11.md`).
- **GTA 6:** list streamers announcing GTA 6 launch streams and pitch them **before 19 Nov**. They'll need editors that week.

## Where to find them

1. **Twitch:** Browse → pick a game (GTA V now, GTA 6 from launch) → sort by viewers → skip the top 20 → open profiles in the 50–2,000
   viewer range.
2. **Kick:** same method. Many Kick streamers have nobody clipping them.
3. **YouTube Live:** search "GTA live Hindi" or "GTA 6 live" and filter by Live.
4. **Check each one:** do they have a YouTube channel? How often do they post Shorts, and how many views do they get? Is there a Discord
   link or "business inquiries" email in their panels or bio?
5. **Discord:** join from their panel link, read `#rules`, and look for `#clipping`, `#clippers`, `#editors`, `#jobs` or `#business`
   channels.

## Discord etiquette (or you get banned)

- **Read the rules first.** Many servers ban self-promotion and unsolicited DMs.
- Use the **business email** from their Twitch or YouTube "About" page, or the server's **official** clipping or editor channel.
- If you DM, send **one** message to the streamer or a mod, with proof (clips you already made). No follow-up for 4–5 days, and only
  one follow-up in total.
- Never spam several servers with the same message.

## The pitch: show, don't ask

1. **Make 2–3 sample Shorts from their own recent stream** before contacting them. Use the account kit graphics, captions and
   zoom-punch. Upload them **unlisted** on YouTube (or send them on Drive). Don't post them publicly on your page without permission.
2. Send the pitch:

**Discord / DM:**
```
Hey {name}! I edited 3 Shorts from your stream on {date} — here they are: {links}
Your Shorts post {X times a week} right now. I can turn every stream into 5 Shorts + 1 YouTube highlight video,
delivered within 24h. First week at {price} so you can see the results. Interested?
```

**Email (subject: "3 free Shorts from your {date} stream"):**
```
Hi {name},
I'm {your name}, a gaming editor. I cut 3 vertical Shorts from your {game} stream on {date}: {links}
For GTA 6 launch week I can deliver daily: 5 Shorts/Reels + 1 long highlight video per stream, 24–48h turnaround.
Starter price: {package price}. Happy to do a paid 1-week trial first.
— {name} | {portfolio link}
```

3. **Limit free work** to the 2–3 samples. After that, a **paid trial week**, then a monthly package.

## Deal terms (write them down; a Discord message or Google Doc is enough)

- **Deliverables:** for example 5 Shorts + 1 long video per stream.
- **Turnaround:** 24–48 hours after the VOD is available.
- **Revisions:** 2 rounds included.
- **Footage:** they share VOD files or links on **Google Drive**. Drive is connected here, so Claude can read the files directly.
  YouTube downloads are blocked in this workspace.
- **Rights:** they own the finished videos and post them on **their** channels. You can show them in your portfolio.
- **Payment:** **50% upfront**, then weekly. PayPal, Wise or Payoneer for foreign clients; UPI or bank transfer for Indian clients.
  Keep records, because it's taxable income.

## What Claude handles in each job

- Finds the best moments in the VOD (hook psychology from `whoop-clipper` and `ad-strategist`).
- Cuts vertical Shorts with captions and the Remotion graphics (`WantedHook`, `ResultStamp`, `ZoomPunch`, `Countdown`, `MoneyCounter`).
- Long-form: a 10–20 min highlight edit with chapters, dead air and filler cut. Word-precise cuts need a transcript (ElevenLabs key or
  local Whisper).
- Checks frames before delivery, and writes titles, descriptions and hooks (`ig-reel`, `ig-caption`).

## Avoid

- Clients who want "free trials" again and again, or who pay only "in exposure".
- Anyone asking you to pay upfront for "access" to clipping work.
- Re-uploading a streamer's content to your own page **without permission**. That's a copyright strike.
