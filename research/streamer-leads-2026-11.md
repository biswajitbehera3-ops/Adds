## How to contact the ones without an email
- YouTube channel → **About** → links (Instagram, Discord, business email behind "View email address").
- Discord or Instagram links pinned in **live stream descriptions or chat**.
- Instagram DM with the same handle.
- Follow the Discord rules in `streamer-outreach-playbook.md`: one message, with samples, no spam.
