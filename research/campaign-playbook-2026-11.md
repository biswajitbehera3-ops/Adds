## Per campaign: what to clip and how to edit

### 1. Let Him Mansplain ($1.25/1K on IG, $0.50 on YouTube, min 2,000 views, cap $300 per post, Global)
- **Moments:** a man's blunt confession ("why I stopped dating"), a hot take the host pushes back on, "what men actually want", red flags and green flags.
- **Hook on screen:** "Men are DONE with modern dating 😳", "He said what every man is thinking", "Ladies… is he right?"
- **Edit:** 30–60s, dead air and "umm"s cut, speaker-tracking crop for two people, 1–3 word captions with the key word in green, zoom-punch on the punchline, no music under speech.
- **End:** a question that starts debate ("Agree or disagree?"). Comments push reach. Keep it fair to the podcast, since the brief is about awareness, not rage bait.
- **Post on Instagram first.** YouTube pays only $0.50.

### 2. LTK – Creator Earnings ($1.50/1K on IG, cap $600 per post, content folder provided)
- **Brief goal:** show that people build real businesses on LTK.
- **Moments:** income reveals ("$X last month from my links"), "I quit my 9–5", before and after a creator's journey.
- **Hook:** the money number first, using the **MoneyCounter** overlay. For example: "$12,400 in ONE month… from outfit links".
- **Edit:** 15–35s, stakes-first, money counter on the hook, captions, app or product shots from the folder, the LTK mention the brief requires.

### 3. Call of Duty MW4 Season 01 Trailer ($1.35/1K on IG, cap $1,200 per post, Global)
- **Moments:** the biggest explosion, the best one-liner, the "hero shot".
- **Edit:** 10–20s "aura edit": synced to a phonk or slowed beat, slow motion into a **ZoomPunch** on each hit, minimal text ("MW4 Season 1 goes crazy 🔥"). It should loop cleanly.
- **Timing:** crowded and 69% spent. Post within days or skip it.

### 4. Clash x HOH (about $1.25/1K, $10K, opened today, Global)
- **Moments:** a hard trivia question, the pause, then someone nails it or fails badly. "Kid genius vs 10 NBA fans" moments.
- **Edit:** question on screen, then the **Countdown** overlay ("Can you answer before him?"), then the reveal. Viewers play along and comment their answers.
- **Note:** it's US sports content. It's harder to get views from India, but nobody else is clipping it yet.

### 5. TJR x Insentos — ⚠️ approach with caution
- TJR is a trading influencer with public "fake guru / scam" call-out videos about him.
- **India:** SEBI restricts unregistered "finfluencers" who promote trading or give investment advice. Clipping trading content to Indian
  audiences could be legally risky. Only consider it if the brief is pure entertainment (for example, the Vegas or lifestyle moments) and
  never post trading tips or "how to make money trading" clips.

## The workflow

1. **You:** join the campaign, set up and test your payout method, and download the source files. Upload them here, or share a Google Drive
   link (Drive is connected).
2. **Me:** find the moments. For word-precise captions I need a transcript: either the ElevenLabs key (paid, best) or a free local Whisper
   install (slower).
3. **Me:** cut 5–10 clips in 9:16 with the speaker-tracking crop, captions and the right overlays (MoneyCounter, Countdown, ZoomPunch,
   ContestantTracker), then check frames over the real footage.
4. **Me:** score each hook with `/ig-reel` and write captions and tags with `/ig-caption`, using the campaign's required tag or mention.
5. **You:** post to Reels and Shorts (schedule a batch in Meta Business Suite), submit each link in Content Rewards right away, keep likes
   and comments on, and don't delete or edit posts during the 7-day earning window.
6. **Both:** after 48 hours, check which clips are working, then make more in that format from new moments.

## How the winning clippers work (from the research)

- **Volume:** 3–10 clips a day per campaign, cross-posted to every allowed platform. Per-post caps mean many solid clips beat one viral one.
- **Speed:** they post in the first days of a campaign, before it gets crowded.
- **Hook in the first second:** an all-caps curiosity or stakes line, never a slow intro.
- **Two styles:** story clips (moment plus caption) and emotional or aura edits (music, slow motion, zoom).
- **Series:** "Part 1" captions bring viewers back.
- **Study the leaderboard:** Content Rewards shows top clips and clippers once you join. Copy their format, not their footage.
- **Clean accounts:** no bought views or engagement groups. Bot-scored posts get held, and fraud reverses payouts.
