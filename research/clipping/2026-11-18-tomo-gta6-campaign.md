## Can we do it? (assessment for an India-based account)
1. **40% from USA/UK/Canada/Australia: the hardest rule.**
   - Our channel's audience is mostly India, so its Shorts will almost certainly fail this.
   - It needs a **separate, English, US-targeted clips account**: post at US prime time (about 6–11 PM ET = 4:30–9:30 AM IST), US-style captions and trends, no India-specific references.
   - Their guide "How to Target a Tier 1 Audience" is required reading for non-Tier-1 clippers.
   - **Never fake location** (VPN or bought views): that's a permanent ban.
2. **TikTok is banned in India**, so in practice it's **Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts** only.
3. **No AI:** our Fenrir AI voice, AI art and AI covers **can't be used** here. Use Tomo's own audio and moment, plus our human-made captions and edits.
4. **Footage:** they explicitly allow clipping Tomo's streams, which counts as a written OK, so Tomo can go on our allow-list for this campaign.
   - Downloading his VODs needs your Mac (YouTube blocks downloads from this container).
   - `stream-moments` can find his chat spikes and moments.
5. **Rockstar risk:** paid clipping sits outside Rockstar's fan-content permission (`do-not-post-rules.md`). Stick to funny and gameplay moments: **no story, no cutscenes, no endings**.
6. **Money reality:** $2/1K needs 2,000+ views to count. A $150 cap means 75K views; the whole budget is 500K views across all clippers, so move fast on launch days.

**Verdict:** possible, but only with a separate English clips account aimed at the US (Reels + Shorts), human edits with no AI, and Tomo's clips. Not with our main India channel.
