Campaign Overview + Goal ​
Tomo is a gaming YouTuber with 1M YT subscribers. This campaign is live on the day GTA VI launches, and Tomo will be playing it from day one. Your mission is to clip his best GTA VI moments and get as many new people as possible to discover Tomo and subscribe to his YouTube channel. Tomo streams GTA VI live on YouTube, so his YouTube livestreams are the main source for clips, along with his GTA VI videos.
Pro tip: GTA VI is the most searched game of the year right now. Post fast: the first clips from launch week could get the biggest push from the algorithm. ​
Rules ​
Videos must feature Tomo and be sourced mainly from his GTA VI YouTube livestreams, or his GTA VI videos.
CTA Requirement: every clip must mention Tomo and tell viewers where to watch more, for example "subscribe to Tomo on YouTube".
Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts only. Cross-posting to all three is encouraged.
Audience Requirement: videos must be in English, and at least 40% Tier 1 (USA, UK, Canada, Australia). Once your video hits 2,000 views, send a screenshot or screen recording of your audience analytics (country breakdown) to the support chat on our Discord. Videos without this proof won't be approved.
Caption Requirement: use #tomovids and #gta6, and mention Tomo's YouTube channel (@Tomovids) on screen or in the caption.
Captions must be readable without sound.
Likes must be visible on all clips. Clips with hidden likes will be rejected.
Page Requirement: your account should be in a related niche (gaming, GTA, streamers, clips). Off-niche accounts may be rejected.
Engagement Requirement: keep at least a 1% engagement rate.
Clip Duration: every clip must stay live for at least 30 days.
Original edits only: no stolen or reuploaded clips. If your clip is inspired by another, make it meaningfully different. No 1:1 copies, and don't post the same or nearly identical clip more than 5 times.
No engagement bait: no misleading titles, fake context or clickbait thumbnails, and never portray Tomo in a negative light.
No AI: no AI-generated thumbnails or AI-generated content.
No fake engagement: buying, botting or boosting views or engagement gets you banned from every Clipping Nation campaign permanently.
Submit your video link within 30 minutes of posting.
Required reading before your first submission: Approval Guide (how approvals, analytics and payouts work) and, if you clip from outside the USA, UK, Canada or Australia, How to Target a Tier 1 Audience.
No low-effort or slop content. Screen recordings of the streams, random moments or clips with no clear purpose are strictly prohibited. All submissions must be properly edited, engaging and relevant to the campaign. One great clip beats ten rushed ones.
How to Structure Your Video ​
Hook: give viewers a reason to keep watching in the first 2 seconds.
The moment: one clear funny, crazy or surprising GTA VI moment. Cut everything that needs more than a few seconds of context.
CTA: mention Tomo's name and tell viewers where to watch more, for example "subscribe to Tomo on YouTube".
The edit, captions and style are up to you, as long as the clip follows the rules above.
What to Look For ​
Funny and chaotic GTA VI moments
First reactions to new features, cars, places and missions
Crazy stunts, chases and fails
Best interactions with other players and characters
Priority format: funny moments and first reactions to GTA VI.
