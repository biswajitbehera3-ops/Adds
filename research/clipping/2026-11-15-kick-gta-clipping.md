## 4. How CLIPPING pays (docs)
- **PayPal, or USDC/USDT on Ethereum mainnet.** Each campaign pays through one method; save it in Payments.
- **Not instant:** paid after the campaign/bounty completes (deadline or budget spent) + staff review + sponsor approval.
- A clip counts only after **>1,000 views**; you must reach the bounty/campaign **minimum on your total** — then **all** counted views pay.
- Per-view bounty: views × rate. Pool bounty: your share of total counted views × pool.
- Rules: no botting/fake engagement, follow the brief, don't hide metrics, no duplicate posts, keep posts public, staff decisions final; breaking them = removal + forfeited earnings.
- Accounts: link TikTok/YouTube/Instagram/X and verify with a code in your bio. India/KYC/fees: not stated in the docs — check in Discord before relying on it.
