## The shared format (all 5)
- **Split screen, 50/50.** Kai's camera on top, cropped tight and zoomed on his face. Gameplay on the bottom, cropped tight on the action.
- **Only one piece of text: a white caption with a black outline at the seam between the two halves.** Either an action tag in asterisks (`*CAUGHT*`, `*CHECKING*`, `*SHOOTS*`, `*USING VAPE*`, `*PS5 TURNS OFF*`) or word-by-word speech captions ("THE HOUSE IS…"). No hook banner, no logo, no extra titles.
- **The title does the selling:** "Kai Cenat [reaction verb] [specific GTA 6 thing]! 😭💀". Kai's name first, one specific thing, emoji at the end.
- **The thumbnail is just a frame** of Kai's shocked face plus the action caption.
