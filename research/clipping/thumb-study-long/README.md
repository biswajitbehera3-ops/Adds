## Style B: short horizontal clips (under 1 min, posted as normal videos)

## Style C: Shorts (vertical, up to 3 min)
A frame from the video: streamer cam on top, gameplay below, **one caption at the seam** (`*NPCS REMEMBERED*`, `WHAT`), sometimes the title on top in white boxes (Speed meets Lucia).

## What this means for us
- Our clips are **vertical, 1–3 min → Shorts (style C)**. Use the strongest frame + one caption, or our designed cover (the e1 BUSTED thumbnail mixes A + C: big shocked face + the game's BUSTED screen).
- If we also post **long horizontal compilations** (e.g. "Kai Cenat's First 17 Hours of GTA 6 – Best Moments"), copy **style A**: giant shocked face cut-out on one side + the single craziest moment on the other + GTA VI logo, 0–3 words, saturated colours, optional red arrow.
- Common to all winners: **one emotion, one moment.** The face is shocked or screaming with the mouth open; the moment is a flip, explosion or plane. No small details, no paragraphs.
