## What it shows
- **Less text = more views.** The top gameplay clips (#1, #3, #4, #5) have one word or no text. The ones with headline banners (#8, #10) or a designed poster (#9) are at the bottom.
- **Cam on top, game on the bottom** is the standard. Typical Gamer uses a smaller framed cam, which leaves more room for the action. That suits a crash or flip clip, where the action is the point.
- **#3 is our truck flip's twin:** a vehicle in the air, 127K views, with no text on the thumbnail.
- **Fan clip channels and streamers' own clip channels both get views.** The 400K+ ones are tied to the streamer (official or a dedicated clips channel).
- **Story scenes (#2) get views, but we don't post them** (our rule).

## Decision for our Kai clip
- Kai's cam on top (tight on his face), the truck below; the caption is the only text.
