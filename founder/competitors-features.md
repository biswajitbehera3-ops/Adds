## Who already shows live chair status

1. **SalonLux**: "Live chair occupancy meters" on an owner command dashboard, plus offline LAN billing. Price is by quote only. https://rmgsoftech.com/products/salonlux/
2. **AXOIX**: "Real-time salon floor display showing chair occupancies and active timers", with per-chair In-Chair / Waiting / Available status in its demo. Officer plan is $18/month for up to 8 chairs. https://axoix.com/industry/salon-spa
3. **BookB**: a live walk-in queue that shows who is waiting and which staff member is free. This is a queue list, not a chair map, and it is priced in GBP. https://www.bookb.io/salon-queue-management
4. **TrimBrim**: a public, customer-facing count of free chairs and the waiting line, which the shop updates by hand with + / −. It is a discovery app, not salon management. https://trimbrim.com/

None of the large players I checked (Zylu, DINGG, MioSalon, Salonist, Zenoti, Fresha, Invoay, SalonBoost, Salonior, ReSpark) states a live chair or floor view on the pages I read. MioSalon's "staff occupancy" calendar and Salonist's waitlist and kiosk check-in are the nearest.

## Pages I could not use
- dingg.app/pricing redirects to a demo form, so there is no public price.
- business.whatsapp.com/products/business-app is not supported by the scraper. I used the whatsapp.com/business/download page and search excerpts instead.
- zenoti.com pricing has no price ("Get a Quote").
- bookb.io/pricing showed only GBP, even with an India location.
- myBillBook is not found in the US Google Play search, so I have no Play rating for it.
- Small salon apps (Zylu, DINGG, ReSpark, SalonBoost, Salonior) show no star rating on Google Play. Only their install counts are visible.
