# Salon app: one page (24 Oct)

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed). It passes on unit margin and fails on year-1 profit with founder pay, on break-even against one person's capacity, and on the old all-India buyer panel. Details in `business-plan.md`.

**The problem.** Owners of busy salons can't see what happens in the salon when they're away: cash goes missing from the drawer, bills get cancelled, customers don't come back. Salon software exists, but its customers' top complaint is that support disappears once they've paid (26 of ~157 public reviews), and setup that never finishes (10).

**The customer.** Salons with 5 or more chairs, good Google reviews and strong local search ranking, starting in Bhubaneswar.

**The offer and price.** The Bhubaneswar Founding Salon Plan: the salon's own app on every phone, the salon live on the owner's phone, cash checks, one-tap WhatsApp bills and reminders; set up in one visit, stylists trained in Odia, Hindi or English, a named person who answers 7 days a week. **₹10,000 setup in two halves** (₹5,000 on setup day; ₹5,000 at day 30 only if stylists bill in the app on 20+ working days, otherwise we fix it or refund the first ₹5,000), **then ₹699 a month, locked 2 years**. First 5 salons: first 3 months free in return for being a reference.

| Number | Value |
|---|---|
| Each paying salon, after its own costs | ₹532 a month (76%) |
| Each new setup, after the visit and expected refunds | ~₹7,500 |
| Break-even on monthly fees alone | 82 salons with ₹40,000 founder pay; 7 without |
| Year 1, setup fees included | +₹2.49 lakh without founder pay; −₹2.31 lakh with ₹40,000 a month |
| Computed year 1 (monthly fees only, with founder pay) | −₹4.69 lakh |
| Cash needed to start | about ₹24,000 (without founder pay) |

**The evidence.**
- Simulated buyers: 20 Bhubaneswar owners of 5+ chair salons went from **3 to 12 of 20** when the plain ₹10,000 + ₹500 offer became the Founding Salon Plan (tested at ₹500 a month, not yet at ₹699). An earlier all-India panel of 100 (1–8 chairs, old price) bought at 9%.
- Competitors: 17 found; paid plans for a salon this size cost ₹1,249–₹4,000 a month. AXOIX and SalonLux already show live chair status, so the edge is the combination plus support that stays, not the live view alone.
- No real customer has been tested yet.

**What the money is for.** Demo phone ₹10,000, first month of visits and print ₹8,000, contingency ₹2,000; the founder's time for demos, setups and support.

**The ask.** No outside money is needed to start if the founder takes no pay in year 1. The next step is the one-phone demo app and the first 40 Bhubaneswar salon visits; the first 5 setups decide whether ₹699 and the guarantee hold with real owners.

_The buyers are simulated and the numbers are projections from estimates (founder pay, salons a month, refunds, churn); real owners and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice._
