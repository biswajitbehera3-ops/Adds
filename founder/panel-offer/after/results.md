# Buyer panel: A salon's own phone app for 5+ chair salons (billing, walk-ins, wallet, one-tap WhatsApp bills and reminders, staff attendance, cash checks, a live salon view for the owner), set up in person per salon

**12 buy · 8 pass** (60% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 339387, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Seeing it running for a few weeks in another busy salon nearby, and hearing from that owner that support really answers in festival weeks.
- Seeing it running in another busy Bhubaneswar salon my size for a few months, with the owner telling me it caught cash going missing that his register didn't.
- Seeing it running smoothly for a few months in another big salon whose owner I know, or my product distributor recommending it.
- Seeing it run on a busy Sunday in another established Bhubaneswar salon my size, and being allowed to keep my register going alongside it for the first month without losing my money back.
- Seeing it run smoothly in a busy salon near me for a month, with that owner telling me his staff were fine with it, and a setup date after the wedding season rush.
- Seeing it run for a few months in a salon whose owner I know or trust, a busy one like mine, and hearing from her that the billing and the cash check really match at night.
- Seeing another established salon owner in Bhubaneswar whose senior staff actually took to it without feeling policed, and being allowed to keep my register running alongside it for the first months.
- Start after the wedding season in a quiet month, bring all my customers across instead of only 500, and give me a backup number so it is not just one person answering.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
