# Board: the salon app (22 Oct)

**Vote: 2 fund if, 1 pass. Average score 3.7 / 10.** (Offers 4 fund if · Product 4 fund if · Monopoly 3 pass)
The lenses apply published frameworks; they are not the authors' opinions.

## Risks more than one member raised
1. **The main promise is not built** (all three). "See from anywhere" and the owner's cash and misuse checks need online sync between phones; today each phone keeps its own data. The buyers most likely to pay (owners often away) are paying for exactly this.
2. **Staff do the work, the owner gets the benefit** (Offers, Product). Selfie plus typing every walk-in at rush hour; if staff skip it, the owner's numbers are wrong.
3. **No way to reach salons yet** (Offers, Monopoly). Sold in person one by one; cost to win a salon unknown; "no owner I know uses it" stops buyers.
4. **Price under pressure** (Offers, Monopoly). ₹999 is at the top of the panel's range; ₹4,999 up front is the top objection.

Where they disagree: Monopoly passes because nothing here is far better than Zylu or MioSalon (not researched yet), so it becomes a price fight. Offers and Product think the cash-leak checks for owners who are away are a real enough pain to fund, with conditions. Product also wants the app cut to one job; Offers wants the stack kept but the risk moved off the buyer.

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)
- [ ] Online sync + cloud backup working, and the owner view stays right when staff phones go offline (all three)
- [ ] Do not say "from anywhere" in the pitch until then (Offers)
- [ ] First market: owners often away, 5-8 chair and multi-branch salons; leave 1-2 chair barbers out (Offers, Monopoly)
- [ ] One-sentence promise; cut what doesn't serve it (Product)
- [ ] Walk-in bill timed at rush hour in a real salon and made fast; mobile number and selfie optional (Offers, Product)
- [ ] Setup fee charged only after the free month if staff still use it, plus a guarantee the CFO can afford (Offers) → /founder-offer
- [ ] 3-5 real pilot salons for a month, leakage found and repeat visits recorded in ₹, owners willing to take calls (Offers, Product)
- [ ] Sunday support and WhatsApp sending owned by someone (Product, Offers)
- [ ] Researched comparison with Zylu and MioSalon showing a large edge on owner-away cash control (Monopoly) → /founder-competitors
- [ ] One city and one channel (distributor or salon referral) with a measured cost to win a salon (Monopoly)
- [ ] Running cost per salon, churn, budget and time known (all) → /founder-cfo

## The strongest version the board can see
Not the pitch as written. A cash-and-staff control app for salon owners who can't be in the salon, sold first to 5-8 chair and two-outlet salons in one city: the owner sees from his phone what was billed, what was cancelled and what cash should be in the drawer, with online sync and backup that make it trustworthy. Staff entry is made as fast as possible (no selfie by default, number optional). The setup fee is charged only once it has caught real leakage in that salon. Pilot salons and a local distributor are the sales channel. The customer-side features (reminders, wallet, referral) become a later or lower-priced plan, not the lead.
