**Total reviews read: about 157** (Play 60, Trustpilot 19, Capterra 63, G2 2, SoftwareSuggest 13).

**Caveats:**
- Public review volume for Indian salon software is thin. Zylu and DINGG have almost no negative reviews in public, and their positive reviews are heavily vendor-solicited.
- Salonist's negative reviews include non-Indian salons. Quotes from those are marked *(non-India)*. A quote is marked *(India)* only when the text itself makes it clear.
- Counts are reviews that mention the theme. One review can count toward several themes.

## What this means (short read)

- **The pain is mostly about the vendor, not the features.** "Support vanishes after payment" and "sales promised more than was delivered" make up the largest cluster by far: 26 + 8 mentions, concentrated on Invoay and Salonist.
- **India-specific complaints:** cloud-only software on flaky internet, and WhatsApp reminders that lag or cost extra on top of the subscription. Both show up in reviews of Indian vendors (MioSalon, DINGG, Zylu).
- **The evidence is thin for Zylu and DINGG.** Their public review profile is almost entirely 5-star and vendor-solicited. Absence of complaints there is not proof of satisfaction.
- **Gaps to fill with primary research:** Reddit (blocked here), YouTube comments (not read) and direct owner interviews. Interviews in particular should test "staff won't use it" and "hidden WhatsApp charges", which have little or no public evidence.
