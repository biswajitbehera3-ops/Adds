# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The market is better chosen. 5+ chair, 4.3★+ salons have money and are easy to find: 86 passed the filters in Bhubaneswar.
- The pain touches the owner's cash daily: leakage, walk-outs, customers who never return.
- Effort and risk are now handled well. One-visit setup, a named person 7 days, and a real guarantee (second ₹5,000 only if stylists bill on 20+ days).
- Round 2's money problem is fixed. One-tap WhatsApp keeps cost near ₹167; each salon leaves ~₹532 a month.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is still unproven.** No paying salon, no real owner, and live sync (the "live salon") is not built. The pitch's best moment cannot be shown yet.
2. **It is sold as the cheapest.** ₹699 sits under every rival (₹1,249–₹4,000), locked 2 years. The real edge is the person who sets up and answers. That edge is one founder's time, and it does not scale.
3. **The ceiling is low at today's pace.** At 3 new salons a month and 3% leaving monthly, the base levels off near 3 ÷ 0.03 = 100 salons. That is about ₹8.4 lakh a year in fees.

## The founder's questions
**3-5 years:** the front desk and cash control for mid-size salons in one region. It needs proof, then people other than the founder doing setup.

**How it earns:** ₹10,000 setup (~₹7,500 kept) plus ₹699 a month (₹8,388 a year; ~₹6,384 kept).

**₹1 crore a year:**
- From fees alone: ₹1,00,00,000 ÷ ₹8,388 ≈ **1,193 paying salons**.
- As profit: ÷ ₹6,384 ≈ 1,566 salons.
- Holding 1,193 at 3% churn means ~36 new salons a month. That is twelve times the year-1 plan.
- At ₹1,999 (Zylu Standard's price) it needs ~417 salons.
- **$1 million:** the exchange rate is not in the brief. It needs ($1M × rate) ÷ ₹8,388 salons, several times the ₹1 crore count.
- What would have to be true: enough 5+ chair salons exist across cities (open question), and a salon can be won and set up without the founder. Churn must stay at or under 3%. Price must rise for new salons once proof exists.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What share of walk-in demos turned into a booked setup?
- How much leakage did the app find in a real salon, in rupees?
- What does it cost to win a salon, in travel and hours?
- Could you charge ₹1,299 and still win, if you had proof?
- Who else could do setup, and what would they cost?
- How many 5+ chair salons are in the next three cities?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Build live sync, then sign 5 real salons and get paid the first ₹5,000 by each.
2. Record real numbers from them: demo-to-yes rate, cost to win, rupees of leakage found, and days staff billed.
3. Turn the first 5 into proof: leakage found, a video, owners who take calls. Raise the price for salon 6 onward. Do not compete on cheapness.
4. Write down the setup and support routine so another person can do it, before going to a second city.
