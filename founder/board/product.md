# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The film tells one experience: the customer who never comes back, the reminder, the referral, the owner's view. That is a story to build back from.
- Setup is part of the product. One visit, the app on every phone, a named person 7 days a week. No competitor found does this.
- Care in the unseen parts: 113 tests, three security rounds, cash-leakage checks.
- The guarantee measures use (20+ billing days), not a signature. That is the right test of the experience.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The shared experience is unbuilt.** Live sync, the live view, backup and the iPhone app all wait on it. No real owner has used the app. Every claim about the experience is untested.
2. **Still no "no".** Billing, wallet, WhatsApp, referrals, attendance, leave, earnings, leakage checks, live view. At least two rivals already show live chairs, so the live view is not the one thing. The pitch still needs a paragraph.
3. **Seams between people.** Reminders and bills need a staff tap. The live view needs the stylist to enter each customer. If staff skip it at rush hour, the owner sees a wrong app.

## The founder's questions
**3–5 years.** The best future is narrow: the tool that brings a salon's customers back (reminder, referral, wallet), done brilliantly, then copied city by city. A full "salon operating system" is where rivals already are.

**How it earns.** Setup ₹10,000 once (~₹7,500 left), plus ₹699 a month (~₹532 left). One salon pays ₹8,388 a year in fees.

**Can it reach ₹1 crore a year?**
- Fees alone: ₹1 crore ÷ ₹8,388 ≈ **1,192 paying salons**.
- Mixed: 500 salons give ~₹41.9 lakh in fees; the other ~₹58 lakh needs ~581 new setups a year (~48 a month).
- Today's plan: 3 new a month with 3% leaving settles near **100 salons** (~₹8.4 lakh a year in fees). Holding 1,192 at 3% churn needs ~36 new salons a month.
- One person supports ~60 salons (`cfo.md`), so 1,192 needs a support team of about 20 people's worth.
- Bhubaneswar has 86 salons passing the filters. 1,192 means many cities. How many 5+ chair salons exist elsewhere, and what one costs to win there, are open questions.
- $1 million is more than ₹1 crore; the exchange rate is not in the brief.

**What would have to be true:** staff use it daily without being chased; churn stays near 3% or lower; setup becomes repeatable by someone other than the founder; the price can rise for bigger salons.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what does it do?
- Which three features would you ship if you could keep only three?
- What does a stylist tap in the first five minutes, with a customer waiting?
- What does the owner see when a stylist forgets an entry?
- What happens when a phone is offline for an hour, or two phones edit one bill?
- Who sets up salon number 61?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Build sync, then run one real 5+ chair salon for 30 days with every stylist on their own phone. Show the live view matching the chairs.
2. Name the one thing in one sentence. Hide every feature that does not serve it in version one.
3. Time real stylists billing at rush hour; publish the real number.
4. Record real visits per setup, real 30-day usage and the real cost to win one salon.
