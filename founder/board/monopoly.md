# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The first market is small and countable: 86 salons passed the filters in Bhubaneswar. Owning most of 86 is a real monopoly-first plan.
- There is a gap: 26 public complaints say support disappears after the sale. Nobody found offers in-person setup with a named person. That is the closest thing to a secret here.
- The economics are good per salon: ~₹532 a month (76%) and ~₹7,500 per setup. Break-even is 7 salons.
- Since round 2, three conditions are done: competitors checked, one city picked, an affordable guarantee.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Distribution is untested.** No paying salon yet. The cost to win one salon is unknown. Every salon needs the founder in person, so growth is capped by one person's days.
2. **No durable edge.** The live floor is not unique (AXOIX sells one at ~₹1,500 a month). Funded rivals could add chair status in weeks. Service is the edge, and service is easy to copy and does not get cheaper with scale.
3. **The core is not built.** Live sync is missing, so the live salon, backup and iPhone app do not exist yet.

## The founder's questions
**3-5 years.** Best case: the default app for top-rated salons in Bhubaneswar, then other similar cities. To last, it must build something rivals lack: data lock-in (wallets, customer history), word of mouth among owners, and maybe links between salons. Today every salon is an island.

**How it earns.** Setup ₹10,000 plus ₹699 a month (₹8,388 a year per salon).

**Can it reach ₹1 crore a year?**
- Monthly fees only: ₹1 crore ÷ ₹8,388 ≈ **1,192 paying salons**.
- With 3% leaving each month, you replace ~36% of salons a year. Each salon then brings ~₹8,388 + ₹3,600 of setups ≈ ₹11,988 a year → **~834 salons**.
- Holding 834 at 3% churn needs ~25 new salons **every month**. The plan has 3. At 3 a month and 3% churn, the base stops near **100 salons**, about ₹12 lakh a year.
- All 86 Bhubaneswar salons would give ~₹7.2 lakh a year in fees. So ₹1 crore needs ~10 cities like it, fully won.
- $1 million: the exchange rate is not in the brief. Use (rate × 1,000,000) ÷ ₹11,988 salons. That count is an open question until the rate is fixed.
- Open questions: how many 5+ chair salons each city has, and the real cost and churn outside Bhubaneswar.

**What would have to be true:** a sales channel that does not need the founder (distributors, paid local setters); churn well below 3%; and ~25 setups a month.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many of the 86 can you sign in 6 months, and what does each cost?
- What do you know about these owners that Zylu and AXOIX do not?
- Who sets up salon 30 if you are at salon 29?
- What gets stronger with each new salon?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Build live sync and run it in the first 5 salons. Show stylists billing on 20+ days.
2. Record the real time and cost to win each salon. Sign 15 of the 86.
3. Test one channel that does not need the founder (a distributor) and price it.
4. Measure real churn over 3 months.
5. Name one advantage that grows with more salons, and start it.
