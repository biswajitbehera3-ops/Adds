# Finding the first salons (Bhubaneswar)

`./run.sh` starts the Google Maps scraper (`tools/google-maps-scraper-kit`), searches 33 terms (3 kinds of salon × 11 areas in two walkable clusters), keeps 4.3★+, 150+ reviews, phone listed, no chains, then ranks for "likely to accept" and writes `founder/leads/first-20-salons.pdf` for the phone.

Ranking (signals from the buyer panel, `founder/PANEL-FEEDBACK.md`): second branch +30 (owners often away bought most), Instagram +20 (young tech owners), 300–1,000 reviews +15, 4.5★+ +10, unisex/family +10, open 7 days +5, no booking system +10, salon software on the website −25 (owners who tried an app bought 0%).

Clusters: A = Saheed Nagar, Jaydev Vihar, Nayapalli, Kharavela Nagar, Bapuji Nagar, Unit 9; B = Patia, Chandrasekharpur, Infocity, Rasulgarh, Sailashree Vihar.

`founder/leads/` is git-ignored: the lists hold phone numbers (personal data, DPDP Act). One-to-one outreach only.
