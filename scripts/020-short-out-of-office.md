# 020 — Short: "The $1 Billion GTA 6 Sick Day" (v2, 14 Nov 2026)

**Concept (our own take, v2):** the Short is a **receipt**. It opens on the bill — what one day of GTA 6 costs the economy — then itemises
who's skipping, who signed off, and ends on the twist (the economy won't notice; your boss will, because it's 80 hours, not one day).
New order and new style compared with the source script: money first, people second, the real cost last.
(v1, the "out of office email" version, is in git history.)

## Voice-over (exact, ~150 words)
> Here's the bill for GTA 6 launch day. One billion dollars. That's one estimate of what America loses when people skip work on November
> nineteenth. So who's skipping? A payroll company tracking three point four million workers says leave requests for that day are up
> thirteen percent. In its survey, nearly one in five workers plan to take the day off. For Gen Z, it's one in three. And some employers
> just signed the form. A US Army battalion is giving a four-day pass to soldiers who re-enlist. A California car shop is closing for the
> day. Now the twist. A billion dollars sounds massive. But it's only about one percent of a single day of America's output. The economy
> will barely notice. Your boss will. Because a Rockstar boss says his own playthrough took eighty hours. That's two weeks of work, not
> one day. So who's really paying the bill?
