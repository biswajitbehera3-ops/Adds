**Facts (Tier 1–2 only):**
- Rockstar has **not** published an official story length (CONFIRMED by absence: no Newswire / Support figure as of 16 Nov).
- Rob Nelson (Rockstar North co-studio head): his own play-through in February took **about 80 hours**, "the main story and some optional
  goals with narrative ramifications"; quote: "I hope that there will be people that want to get up, have a cup of coffee, shower, get
  dressed, go out for a little exercise… interact with some people, explore, maybe get into some trouble. Rob some places, make some
  money." → REPORTED · The New York Times, 27 Aug 2026.
- Jason and Lucia start small (low-end cars, basic locks) and work up to high-value jobs; the game "strongly encourages" playing
  in-character; "the relationship is yours to nurture or yours to lose" → REPORTED · The Guardian, 15 Nov 2026.
- 62 types of birds to spot with binoculars → REPORTED · The Guardian.
- Up to **212 GB** free space → CONFIRMED · Rockstar Newswire (3 Nov). Unlock = **midnight local time**, India 12:00 AM IST 19 Nov →
  CONFIRMED · Rockstar Support / PlayStation Blog.
- **Not used:** the 50-hour / 100-mission / 5-chapter / 200-hour numbers (from leaked review copies). The script says they exist and
  why we skip them, without repeating them.

**Footage needed:** Launch Trailer, Trailer 2, Extended Look (official); screenshots of the viral headlines (blur outlet logos except
the word "officially"); NYT and Guardian headline cards (text only, rebuilt in our style).
