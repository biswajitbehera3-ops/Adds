## [0:00–0:25] HOOK
*On screen: Extended Look police chase, freeze on six stars, red "CAUGHT" stamp (ResultStamp, fail).*

In GTA 5, losing the cops was easy. Break line of sight, wait for the stars to go, and you were free — same car, same clothes,
same gun. In GTA 6, that's how you get caught. Rockstar rebuilt the police from scratch, and the cops now remember things about
you. So here are ten GTA 5 habits you need to drop before November 19th.

## [0:25–1:00] #10 — DOING CRIMES IN FRONT OF EVERYONE
*On screen: "#10", a robbery shot from the trailers; pop-up "WITNESS = REPORT".*

In GTA 6, a crime doesn't magically alert every cop in the city. According to Rob Nelson, it only gets reported if someone
**witnesses it or an alarm goes off**. That flips the old habit around: in GTA 5 it didn't matter who saw you. Now witnesses are
the problem — and so are alarms. Pick quiet moments, and you might never get a star at all.

## [1:00–1:35] #9 — IGNORING CAMERAS
*On screen: "#9", zoom into a CCTV camera in a store shot; icon "CCTV" lights up.*

Cameras now matter. Edge's preview confirmed the police track **CCTV footage** as part of what they know about you. So that
camera in the corner of the store isn't decoration anymore — it's evidence. If you're robbing somewhere, think about the cameras
before you think about the cash.

## [1:35–2:10] #8 — WALKING AWAY IN THE SAME CLOTHES
*On screen: "#8", outfit change montage; icon "CLOTHING" crosses out.*

In GTA 5, your outfit never mattered to the police. In GTA 6, the heat system tracks the **clothing you wore during the crime**.
Change your clothes, and you're harder to recognise. Same robbery, same cops — but a different jacket can be the difference
between walking past them and getting stopped.

## [2:10–2:40] #7 — KEEPING THE SAME GUN OUT
*On screen: "#7", weapon icon; Jason holstering.*

The police also track the **weapon used or carried**. So running around with the gun you just used is basically wearing a name tag.
Edge reported that abandoning the weapon removes another identifying detail. Put it away — or get rid of it.

## [2:40–3:15] #6 — STAYING TOGETHER AFTER A DUO JOB
*On screen: "#6", Jason and Lucia split screen, "2 SUSPECTS" → "1 SUSPECT".*

This one's brand new. If witnesses report **two suspects**, the police know they're looking for a pair. Rockstar's answer: Jason
and Lucia can **split up** to make that description less accurate. In GTA 5 there was no "couple" to look for. In GTA 6, being seen
together is a clue.

## [3:15–3:50] #5 — STEALING THE FIRST CAR YOU SEE
*On screen: "#5", car-jack shot; three pop-ups: "ARMED DRIVER", "TRACKER", "EMPTY TANK".*

In GTA 5, any car was a getaway car. In GTA 6, a random stolen car can come with three surprises: an **armed driver**, a **vehicle
tracking system**, or an **almost empty fuel tank** — because every vehicle now has a fuel gauge and an engine-health indicator.
Plan your getaway car before the job, not during the chase.

## [3:50–4:25] #4 — THINKING FIVE STARS IS THE MAX
*On screen: "#4", six stars animate in; then hollow stars.*

GTA 5 topped out at five stars. GTA 6 brings back the **six-star wanted level**. And the stars tell you more now — **hollow stars**
mean the police know a crime happened but don't know who did it. In the Extended Look, Lucia drives straight past cops with hollow
stars and they do nothing. Learn to read the stars, and you'll know when you're actually safe.

## [4:25–5:00] #3 — RUNNING STRAIGHT DOWN THE ROAD
*On screen: "#3", spike strip shot; K-9 icon.*

The cops have new tools. In Edge's demo, officers talked over the radio and laid a **spike strip** across the road. And police
**dogs** can now play a direct role in catching suspects. So the classic "floor it down the main road" escape is riskier than ever.

## [5:00–5:35] #2 — ALWAYS ESCAPING BY CAR
*On screen: "#2", bus shot; quote card from Phil Hooker.*

Here's the smartest new trick: **public transport**. GTA 6 has working buses and trains. And Rockstar North's Phil Hooker told Edge:
*"If they didn't see you get on, and then you get out of the wanted area, why would they search the bus unless they see you get
onto it?"* So your best getaway vehicle might be a city bus.

## [5:35–6:10] #1 — PLAYING LIKE A PSYCHO
*On screen: "#1", angel ↔ devil scale animating from Professional to Irredeemable.*

The biggest change: GTA 6 has a **Criminal Profile** that rates Jason and Lucia from **Professional** all the way to
**Irredeemable**, with angel and devil icons — and Rockstar says it **influences how their story unfolds**. People in the world
also remember how you've treated them. GTA 5 never judged you. GTA 6 does. Bonus tip from Rob Nelson: blowing a safe open with
explosives can damage the valuables inside — so even the loot rewards a cleaner job.

*On screen: "WHICH HABIT IS HARDEST TO DROP?", subscribe card.*

GTA 6 wants you to think like a real criminal, not just outrun the cops. Which GTA 5 habit is going to be the hardest for you to
