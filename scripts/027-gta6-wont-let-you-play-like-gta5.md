## Facts (Tier 1–2 only)
- **Body system.** REPORTED · Dazed, Rockstar developers interview, 26 Aug 2026 (via Kotaku):
  - Jason's and Lucia's bodies change with what they do and eat. Eat too much and they put on weight.
  - Working out sheds pounds and builds muscle tone.
  - Not sleeping, running from the cops too long, or "multi-day benders" change their faces and bodies.
- **Gym membership exists.** REPORTED · Edge.
- **Weapon carry limit.** REPORTED · GameByte, 2 Nov 2026, citing hands-on previews:
  - up to **4 weapons** on you: **2 concealed sidearms + 2 long guns**;
  - the rest of your arsenal sits in **your vehicle's trunk**;
  - GameByte also reports Rob Nelson pointed to Red Dead Redemption 2 as the model.
- **How you carry matters.** REPORTED · Edge:
  - people react differently to a gun **on your back** than to one **in your hands**;
  - carrying doesn't automatically give you a wanted level, and you can put it away before it escalates.
- **Your partner drives while you shoot.** REPORTED · Edge.
- **You start small:** cheap cars, basic locks, then bigger jobs. REPORTED · The Guardian, 15 Nov 2026.
- **Police know you, not just where you are.** Callback to 006:
  - a crime is reported only if someone sees it or an alarm goes off (GameSpot; Rob Nelson via Inven Global);
  - the report tracks your description, clothes, CCTV and weapon, and you can lower it by changing clothes or splitting up (Edge).

**Do NOT say:**
- that the gym raises "strength/stamina stats", or that sleep lowers "attributes" (not in Dazed's write-up);
- that Jason can pass Lucia his gun;
- tsunamis, volcanoes or hurricanes;
- "only 1–2 guns" (the report says 4);
- that a mask is confirmed to clear your face (GameSpot only *thinks* so).

**Footage needed:** Trailer 1, Trailer 2, Launch Trailer, Extended Look (official), Newswire screenshots. Also our illustrations:
weight-scale bar, Zzz meter, trunk loadout, police report card.
