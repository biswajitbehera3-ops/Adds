**Angle (fresh):** big channels are on "everything you need to know" lists. This video does the awards story as a mystery:
- How can a game nobody has played be up for Game of the Year?
- Who has actually played it?
- What does this tell us about reviews?
- Why is Lucia's actress named but not Jason's?

**Facts (Tier 1–2 only):**
- **The Game Awards nominations:** GTA 6 got **8 nominations, including Game of the Year**. Nominees were revealed on a livestream by Geoff Keighley on Tue 17 Nov. REPORTED · Variety.
- **The other GOTY nominees:** 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. REPORTED · Variety.
- **Nominations are chosen by a jury** of 100+ media outlets and creators. REPORTED · Variety.
- **Eligibility deadline:** 20 Nov 2026, 9 PM PT. The ceremony is at the Peacock Theater, Los Angeles. REPORTED · Forbes.
- **Resident Evil Requiem swept the Golden Joystick Awards.** REPORTED · Forbes (citing Vice).
- **Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos.** She was revealed through a Best Performance nomination, and IGN says she has confirmed she voices Lucia. Jason's actor was not nominated and is still unknown. REPORTED · IGN.
- **Lucia is the first playable female lead in a 3D GTA.** REPORTED · BBC ("first playable female character in any of the 3D games").
- **Pre-orders:** over $1.4B, 87% Ultimate Edition. REPORTED · GamesIndustry.biz (a Sensor Tower estimate).
- **India unlock:** 12:00 AM IST, 19 Nov. CONFIRMED · Rockstar Support.

**Do NOT say:**
- that reviews are out, or any review date (not confirmed);
- any Metacritic number (the "mixed" headline is 5 pre-release user scores);
- who plays Jason;
- that Rockstar paid for nominations (a comment-section claim, no evidence);
- GTA 6's other six categories (unverified; check The Game Awards' page and add them if confirmed).
