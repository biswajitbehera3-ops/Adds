## THE SCRIPT

### 1. HOOK (0:00–0:20)
"In twenty-four hours, GTA 6 broke a twenty-eight-year record, and you won't believe what broke next.
Thirteen years of waiting, and in one day it hit the top of Metacritic, sold an estimated seventeen million copies, and knocked a chunk of PlayStation offline.
Here's everything that happened on launch day, in order. No story spoilers. I promise."

### 2. MIDNIGHT (0:20–0:55)
"It didn't start in America. It started in New Zealand, at 4:30 in the afternoon, Indian time. Then the unlock rolled around the planet like a wave, midnight by midnight.
India got it at twelve AM sharp. The UK at five-thirty in the morning, our time. And America? Still waiting. The East Coast only gets in at ten-thirty this morning.
So for once, India played GTA before most of America did."

**[ON SCREEN:** a world map with a midnight line sweeping east→west, city chips lighting up: AUCKLAND 4:30 PM · MUMBAI 12:00 AM · LONDON 5:30 AM · NEW YORK 10:30 AM (IST) · SourceTag CONFIRMED · Rockstar Support]

### 3. THE SCORE (0:55–1:50)
"Then the reviews landed, and they were ridiculous.
Ninety-nine on Metacritic. For context, GTA 5 got a ninety-seven, and that was already one of the best-reviewed games ever made.
A ninety-nine ties one game: Zelda: Ocarina of Time, from 1998. That's it. In the whole history of Metacritic, those two are at the top. IGN gave it a perfect ten.
And here's the funny bit. Both of them are now up for Game of the Year at the same awards, twenty-eight years apart.
But it's not perfect, and I'm not going to pretend it is. Critics had two real complaints. One: it runs at thirty frames per second on consoles. Two: some of the missions play it safe, the way older GTA missions do.
So: the best world ever built, with missions that don't always take the same risks."

**[ON SCREEN:** a 97 → 99 counter · two cards "1998 · 99" vs "2026 · 99" · "IGN 10/10" · amber chips "30 FPS" · "SAFE MISSIONS" · SourceTag REPORTED · Reuters / IGN / review roundups]

### 4. THE MONEY (1:50–2:45)
"Now the money, because the numbers are hard to even picture.
Sensor Tower, a company that tracks app and game sales, estimates around seventeen million copies sold by Wednesday. That's more than one and a half billion dollars, before most of America had even played it.
Forbes says pre-orders alone were close to one point seven billion.
To put that in rupees: that's around fourteen thousand crore. In about a day.
And the game reportedly cost over a billion dollars to make. So Rockstar made its money back, plus some, before lunch.
One NYU professor told Reuters it's 'the Star Wars of this generation'. Honestly? On numbers alone, it beats Star Wars."

**[ON SCREEN:** a cash counter to "$1.65B+" · "≈ ₹14,000 CRORE" · "17M (ESTIMATE)" chip · "$1B+ TO MAKE" · SourceTag REPORTED · Reuters / Sensor Tower / Forbes]

### 5. THE CRASH (2:45–3:40)
"And then PlayStation started to wobble.
First, the warning. The night before, PlayStation's own support account posted that PS5 players needed a system update before the game would run. Nine million people saw that post.
Lots of people didn't. They hit play at midnight and got an update screen instead.
Then, as the launch rolled across the world, thousands of players reported PlayStation Network outages. The Hollywood Reporter and Yahoo both covered it.
If you're stuck right now, here's what to do. Check PlayStation's status page first. Install the system update. Restart. And if PSN itself is down, the problem isn't you. Just wait it out.
America unlocks at ten-thirty this morning, Indian time, so expect a second wave."

### RETENTION BEAT (3:40–3:50)
"But the craziest part of launch day wasn't the game. It was what people did to get the day off."

### 6. THE WORLD TOOK THE DAY OFF (3:50–4:45)
"In America, CBS found people writing 'GTA 6' on their leave requests. Not 'personal reasons'. Just: GTA 6.
In Belfast, a school wrote to parents because launch day clashes with GCSE maths exams. Their line: 'Grand Theft Auto can wait until the weekend.' Pupils who turned up went into a draw for a seventy-pound gift card.
One Catholic school went further. It's paying students about eighty-five dollars just to come to class.
And a player in Sydney told Reuters: 'I've waited thirteen years, man. Thirteen years.'
Fair enough."

"And the players who did get in? The thing everyone's talking about isn't a mission. It's the phone.
The top post on the GTA 6 subreddit right now is someone just scrolling: a social feed, people's profiles, comments, texts, the news, the weather. You can actually doom-scroll in GTA.
There's a Reminders app. The GPS talks you through every single turn.
And one thing is gone. Polygon noticed that a feature GTA has had for twenty-five years, the sex-worker NPCs, just isn't in this one."
