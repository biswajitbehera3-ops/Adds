# 008 — Shorts (v2): one subject each, 45–50 seconds

Each Short is about **one subject only** and stands on its own (no "as I said earlier"). Format: 9:16 (1080×1920), voice = Bram,
GTA neon karaoke subtitles (same as 006), CONFIRMED / REPORTED tag on every fact, sources in the Short's description. Opening
lines scored with `yt-script/hookscore.py`. Runtime = spoken words ÷ 150 per minute.

**Vertical framing (all four):** trailer footage is 16:9, so a centre crop keeps about a third of the width: faces and cars stay,
wide skyline edges go (fine). Every chart, counter and quote card is built **natively vertical** in Remotion, never cropped from
16:9. Keep text inside the middle 1080×1420 (the Shorts buttons cover the bottom ~300 px and the right edge).

## Short 1 — GTA 6 broke an internet record (~48 s)
**Title:** `GTA 6 Broke an Internet Record Before Launch #GTA6`
**On screen, first 2 s:** `NOT EVEN OUT YET` (white) → `RECORD BROKEN` (red stamp)

**Spoken:**
> Before you pre-load GTA 6 tonight, know this: the downloads already broke a UK internet record.
> On Tuesday the tenth, pre-loads opened. Virgin Media says its network hit the biggest traffic peak in its history. It beat the
> old record, set during an Arsenal Champions League match, by almost four percent. By eleven-oh-five that night, it had moved more
> data than on any full day before. In the first hour alone, traffic jumped fifty-one percent.
> Another UK provider, YouFibre, told customers their speeds had dropped because of GTA 6 downloads.
> In America, Xfinity is boosting customers' speeds, and calls this launch "the biggest moment in internet history".
> And remember, this is only the pre-load. Launch night hasn't even happened.

**On screen, beat by beat:**
- EL highway at night (centre crop, light streaks) → date chip "TUE 10 NOV · PRE-LOAD OPENS".
- Vertical bar pair: "ARSENAL v ATLÉTICO (old record)" vs "GTA 6 PRE-LOAD +3.8%" — the GTA bar edges past with a hit.
- A clock spinning to "11:05 PM" with the caption "MORE DATA THAN ANY FULL DAY".
- Big counter "+51.6%" with caption "FIRST HOUR".
- Email-style card (our own design, not a screenshot): "YouFibre: speeds down due to GTA 6 downloads".
- Quote card: Xfinity — "the biggest moment in internet history".
- End on a frozen download bar at 99% — "LAUNCH NIGHT: 19 NOV".
- Tags: REPORTED · Virgin Media O2 (press release, 11 Nov) / Forbes / Comcast.

**Loop:** "Launch night hasn't even happened." → "Before you pre-load GTA 6 tonight…"
**Don't say:** "the internet crashed" (record traffic, not an outage); an exact file size (Virgin Media says 190 GB, Forbes says
212 GB on console — they disagree).

## Short 2 — The "10 million" pre-orders (~49 s)
**Title:** `Did GTA 6 Really Sell 10 Million? #GTA6`
**On screen, first 2 s:** `10,000,000?` (gold) → `NOT OFFICIAL` (red stamp)

**Spoken:**
> Before you say GTA 6 sold ten million, know this: nobody official said that number.
> It comes from Sensor Tower, a company that estimates sales from store data. On November seventh, their estimate was nearly
> eight million pre-orders. By the tenth, GameSpot reported nine point seven million. Now NDTV says ten point three million,
> around a billion dollars. Three in every four are on PlayStation 5.
> If that's right, GTA 6 has passed Cyberpunk 2077's record of eight million. But Rockstar hasn't confirmed a single number.
> So what is confirmed? Sony says it's the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history. Xbox says it's the most
> pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store. No numbers. Just two words. All time.

**On screen, beat by beat:**
- Counter racing to 10,000,000 → red stamp.
- Vertical timeline climbing: "7 NOV · ~8M" → "10 NOV · 9.69M" → "11 NOV · 10.3M" (REPORTED · Sensor Tower via GamesIndustry.biz /
  GameSpot / NDTV), with "ESTIMATE" on each step.
- Four controller icons, three blue (PS5), one green (Xbox).
- Two-bar race: CYBERPUNK 2077 8M vs GTA 6 ~10M; then a grey stamp "ROCKSTAR: NO NUMBER".
- Two stacked quote cards, PlayStation Blog and Xbox Wire, with "ALL TIME" in gold (CONFIRMED).
- Footage under the cards: EL night city, T1 beach crowd (centre crop).

**Loop:** "Just two words. All time." → "nobody official said that number."

## Short 3 — GTA 6 vs Hollywood (~48 s)
**Title:** `Why Hollywood Is Worried About GTA 6 #GTA6`
**On screen, first 2 s:** `GTA 6 vs HOLLYWOOD`

**Spoken:**
> Before you call GTA 6 just a game, know this: Hollywood is bracing for it.
> The Hollywood Reporter says the new Hunger Games film opens the day after GTA 6 launches. So do new seasons of Yellowjackets and
> Percy Jackson. That's bad timing.
> The game's first-year sales forecast is forty-five million copies and three point two billion dollars. That's more than Avengers:
> Endgame made at the box office.
> Geoff Keighley, the host of The Game Awards, says it will be the biggest entertainment launch in history.
> And an entertainment expert at PwC says streaming will be hit hardest, because GTA 6 fights for the same TV in your living room.
> He thinks the pull could last all through December.

**On screen, beat by beat:**
- Official artwork Jason & Lucia Rooftop (vertical crop on the two of them).
- Vertical release calendar: "19 NOV · GTA 6" glowing pink, "20 NOV · HUNGER GAMES · YELLOWJACKETS · PERCY JACKSON" in grey
  (text only; no film posters or logos).
- Two-bar comparison: "AVENGERS: ENDGAME · BOX OFFICE" vs "GTA 6 · YEAR 1 FORECAST $3.2B" (label FORECAST clearly).
- Quote card: Geoff Keighley — "the biggest entertainment launch in history".
- A TV-shaped frame: a streaming-style bar empties while a GTA 6 shot fills the screen. Caption "STREAMING HIT HARDEST — PwC".
- End on EL skyline at night with "PULL COULD LAST THROUGH DECEMBER".
- Tags: REPORTED · The Hollywood Reporter (11 Nov).

**Loop:** "…all through December." → "Before you call GTA 6 just a game…"
**Don't say:** whose forecast it is (THR doesn't name it) — say "the forecast", tagged REPORTED · THR.

## Short 4 — Taking the day off (~46 s)
**Title:** `This Company Gave 7,000 People the Day Off for GTA 6 #GTA6`
**On screen, first 2 s:** `19 NOV = DAY OFF?`

**Spoken:**
> Before you ask your boss for the GTA 6 day off, know this: Logitech already said yes to seven thousand people.
> Logitech, the company behind gaming mice and keyboards, has named November nineteenth Logi Game Day. Everyone, from executives
> to interns, gets the day off. They can play GTA 6, another game, or nothing at all.
> And they're not alone. A Virgin Media poll of UK players found half of working players plan to take time off for GTA 6. A third
> expect to take at least two days. And players expect to spend seventeen hours in the game in the first week.
> So, be honest. Are you taking the day off, or playing after work?

**On screen, beat by beat:**
- A calendar flips to "19 NOVEMBER", stamped "LOGI GAME DAY" (text only, no Logitech logo).
- People-icon grid filling to "7,000" with labels "EXECUTIVES → INTERNS".
- Three choice chips: "GTA 6" · "ANOTHER GAME" · "NOTHING".
- Poll cards: "50% TAKING TIME OFF" · "1 IN 3: TWO DAYS+" · "17 HOURS IN WEEK ONE" (REPORTED · Virgin Media O2 poll, 520 UK
  players, 30 Oct).
- Footage: T2 0:39 beach/lifestyle shots, centre crop.
- End on the comment-question card: "DAY OFF or AFTER WORK?"
- Tags: CONFIRMED · Logitech press release / REPORTED · Kotaku.

**Loop:** "…or playing after work?" → "Before you ask your boss…"
