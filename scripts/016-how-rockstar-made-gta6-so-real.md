## Facts (all REPORTED · The Guardian, Keza MacDonald, 11 Nov 2026, unless noted)
- First Miami research trips in **2015**. Rob Nelson: "Before you're doing hyper-organised research trips, you're immersing yourself in the place and its history, the vibe of it."
- Nelson: "We're always semi-undercover… But if you have a bunch of dorks going through a terrifying neighbourhood, you do stick out a bit."
- The setting always comes first, before the story.
- Detail: the clothes pedestrians wear, street art, flyers on cafe walls, the insides of fridges.
- A team of **almost 5,000 people**; a small group of long-time decision-makers.
- Jason eats a hot dog; at a gas station he takes bites of a donut with one hand while sipping a beer with the other.
- Nelson got "briefly upset" during a jewellery-store stick-up when a security guard's foot placement didn't look right.
- Jennifer Kolbe on GTA IV research: people who'd lived in New York all their lives "were going to places they would never go to… We then give that experience to players."
- Nelson on a GTA outside America: "Never say never, but there's still so much to do in America."
- Rockstar's Instagram reshared OnlyInDade's GTA 6 clips that people mistook for real Miami (REPORTED · NBC Miami).
- Skinny Louie restaurants in Florida became Burger Shot from 13 Nov to 31 Dec (REPORTED · IGN).
