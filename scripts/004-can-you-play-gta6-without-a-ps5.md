# 004 — Can You Play GTA 6 Without a PS5? — Production script v1

**Standard:** `production/style-guide.md` · **Runtime:** ~5:50 · **VO:** ~880 words · **Music:** synthwave/Miami-night (energy) →
dark pulse (the catch) → soft pads (outro)

**Title (pick one, ≤60 chars):**
- A: `Can You Play GTA 6 Without a PS5? (Yes, But…)`
- B: `GTA 6 on Xbox One?! Every Way to Play, Ranked`

**Facts (all Tier 1, 10 Nov 2026):**
- Launch 19 Nov 2026, PS5 + Xbox Series X|S; no PC at launch — Rockstar Newswire.
- Targets **30 frames per second** on both platforms — Rockstar Newswire.
- **PS5 Pro:** PSSR clarity + ray-traced shadows, "only available on PlayStation 5 Pro" — Rockstar Newswire.
- **PS5:** DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D Audio, improved load times — "play best on PlayStation 5 consoles" — Rockstar Newswire.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming** on **Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S**, from **1 AM ET, 19 Nov**, using **Series S graphics settings**; "connection
  speeds and other technical factors may influence game performance" — Rockstar Newswire + Xbox Wire.
- From **11 Nov**, players "in supported markets" can **buy cloud playtime without a Game Pass subscription** — Xbox Wire.
- **Remote Play** supported on PlayStation and Xbox — Rockstar Newswire.
- GTA V, RDR2 coming to Xbox Cloud "in the coming months" — Xbox Wire.

**Do NOT say:** that cloud works on phones, PCs or TVs (Xbox Wire names only Xbox One and Series X|S); that India is or isn't a
supported market (not stated — **before recording, check Xbox's supported-countries page yourself** and add one line if needed); any
price for cloud playtime (not announced in our sources).

**Footage needed:** Launch Trailer, Trailer 2, Extended Look (official); Rockstar Newswire PS5 / Xbox images; Xbox Wire cover art.
Console product shots: draw them as simple flat icons (our own graphic) — don't use Sony/Microsoft marketing photos.
