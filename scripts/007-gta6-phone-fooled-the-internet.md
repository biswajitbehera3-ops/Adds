**Facts (Tier 1–2 only):**
- Rockstar posted a GTA 6 gameplay clip as a **Story on its official Instagram** (Wed 11 Nov) recreating a viral real-life Miami
  video of parked cars; some people thought it was real footage; Rockstar liked the original poster's (OnlyInDade) post — **Kotaku**.
- In-game social media: Trailer 1 shows phone-style posts and a livestream (e.g. 0:42–0:57, "LIVE"); the Extended Look shows a
  **"LIVE 51k"** stream overlay while driving (18:24) and a bystander filming on his phone (18:12) — **Rockstar footage** (we show it,
  we don't over-interpret it).
- Edge's preview: Jason and Lucia use an **app that lists vehicles wanted by fences**, with each car's model, security and tracking;
  some high-end cars transmit their location to police; fences have signal-blocking at drop-off — **REPORTED · Edge**.
- Heat icons include CCTV; a crime is reported if someone **sees it** or an alarm goes off — **REPORTED · Edge / GameSpot**.

**Do NOT say:** that the viral clip was "filmed on Jason and Lucia's in-game phones" (ResetEra speculation — only as "some fans think");
any feature not in the list above (selfie mode, dating apps, banking apps etc. are unconfirmed); the original creator's real video
(not ours to use — describe it, don't show it).

**Footage:** Rockstar's Instagram Story clip (screen-record before it expires — Rockstar's own published material, used for
commentary); Trailer 1 0:42–0:57 (social posts/stream); EL 18:12–18:28 (phone filmer, LIVE 51k); EL 6:06 (car theft); EL 15:52 (car
swap); T1 0:48 CCTV still.
