## Packaging
- **Title (use this one):** `10 Million Pre-Orders? The GTA 6 Numbers, Explained` — the hook is about the "ten million" number, so
  the title has to promise that number (yt-script rule: the first sentence confirms the title).
- **Backup title:** `GTA 6 Just Broke a Record That Stood for 6 Years` (pairs with hook B below).
- **Thumbnail A:** huge gold "10,000,000" with a small amber stamp "ESTIMATE?", Jason & Lucia official artwork cut out in front.
- **Thumbnail B:** two bars, "CYBERPUNK 8M" (yellow) vs "GTA 6 10M?" (pink), Lucia cut-out on the right.

**Why A:** The Warning fits because the viewer is about to repeat a number in the comments and get corrected. A keeps the question
open (what *did* Sony and Xbox confirm?) and B answers its own question in the second sentence.

## Facts (Tier 1–2 only, labelled)
- **Estimate, not official:** Sensor Tower estimates ~9.69M pre-purchases / ~$942M (GameSpot, updated 10 Nov) and ~10.3M / ~$1B
  (NDTV, 11 Nov). PS5 ≈ 7.23M (75%), Xbox Series ≈ 2.46M (25%) — **REPORTED · Sensor Tower via GameSpot/NDTV**.
- Cyberpunk 2077's pre-order record of 8M (2020) — **REPORTED · GameSpot**.
- **Official:** most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history (PlayStation Blog, 10 Nov); most pre-ordered title of all time on
  the Microsoft Store (Xbox Wire, 10 Nov) — **CONFIRMED**.
- **New today:** Virgin Media (UK) says GTA 6 pre-loads set its biggest-ever traffic peak and highest-ever daily data volume —
  **REPORTED · Forbes / Virgin Media statement**.
- Logitech gives its ~7,000 employees the day off to play — **REPORTED · Kotaku / VGC**.
- The Hollywood Reporter: "biggest entertainment launch in history"; the new Hunger Games film opens the next day — **REPORTED · THR**.
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick: "You compete against everything and nothing… it's our job to make the best entertainment on
  Earth." — **REPORTED · GameSpot via GamesBeat**.

**Do NOT say:** "GTA 6 sold ten million" as fact · any price · that Rockstar or Take-Two confirmed a number · that the internet
"crashed" (Virgin Media said record traffic, not an outage).
