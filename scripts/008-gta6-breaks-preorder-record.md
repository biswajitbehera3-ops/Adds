**Facts (Tier 1–2, with the right label):**
- **Estimate, not official:** Sensor Tower estimates ~9.69M pre-purchases / ~$942M (GameSpot, updated 10 Nov) and ~10.3M / ~$1B
  (NDTV, 11 Nov). PS5 ≈ 7.23M (75%), Xbox Series ≈ 2.46M (25%). Not confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two — **REPORTED · Sensor Tower**.
- If accurate, it beats Cyberpunk 2077's pre-order record of 8M (2020) — **REPORTED · GameSpot**.
- **Official:** most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history (PlayStation Blog, 10 Nov); most pre-ordered title of all time on
  the Microsoft Store (Xbox Wire, 10 Nov) — **CONFIRMED**.
- Logitech is giving its ~7,000 employees the day off for GTA 6 ("Logi Game Day") — **REPORTED · Kotaku / VGC**.
- Hollywood is bracing: The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opens the day after GTA 6 — **REPORTED · The Hollywood Reporter**.
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick: "You compete against everything and nothing… it's our job to make the best entertainment on Earth."
  — **REPORTED · GameSpot via GamesBeat**.
- Festival tickets on general sale since 11 Nov — **CONFIRMED · Rockstar Newswire / REPORTED · USA Today**.

**Do NOT say:** "GTA 6 sold 10 million" as fact (it's an estimate of pre-purchases); any price (rumour only); that Rockstar
confirmed a number.
