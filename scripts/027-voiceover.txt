GTA 6 is watching what you eat, how you sleep and what's in your pockets, and one of those will cost you your first heist.

Ten GTA 5 habits, every one sourced on screen. Then which one costs you, and the rumours I really hope are true. So let's go habit by habit, starting with your body.

Habit one: eating junk for free. Rockstar's developers told Dazed that Jason's and Lucia's bodies change with what you do. Eat too much, and they put on weight. Michael, Franklin and Trevor never gained a pound. Jason and Lucia will.

Habit two: never sleeping. Skip sleep, run from the cops for too long, or go on what they call multi-day benders, and it shows on their faces.

Habit three: skipping the gym. Working out sheds the weight and builds muscle tone, and Edge reports a gym membership. San Andreas fans, the gym is back. Whether it changes your stats too? Nobody's said.

That's your body. But remember the hook: one habit costs you your first heist. It's coming up in the next three.

Habit four: carrying every gun in the game. Hands-on previews report you can carry four. Two hidden handguns, two long guns. GameByte says Rob Nelson pointed to Red Dead 2 as the model.

Habit five: keeping every gun on you. Everything else lives in your car's trunk. So before every job, you choose. Remember that one.

Habit six: thinking nobody notices. Edge reports people react differently to a gun on your back than a gun in your hands. Carrying isn't an instant wanted level, but leave it out and it escalates.

Habit seven: doing it all alone. Edge reports your partner can drive while you shoot. And the Guardian says you start small: cheap cars, basic locks.

Habit eight: one button, any car. Previews report older cars open with a simple tool, newer ones need a key cloner, and keyless ones need a trick that copies the key's signal. Your phone even shows a car's security, its value, and what it costs to register it as yours.

Habit nine: waiting for a mission marker, and expecting cash straight away. Rob Nelson says robbery targets don't get a marker, and small ones aren't even on the map. And loot isn't money until you take it to a fence.

Habit ten: one wrong turn, mission failed. Rob Nelson told IGN the opening mission has more than one way to escape. In his words, a very conscious effort to open things up.

So, which habit costs you your first heist? Your pockets. In GTA 5, you walked in with everything. In GTA 6, you walk in with four guns, and the rest is in a trunk. Now add the last thing: a crime gets reported when someone sees it. Edge says the police report tracks your face, your clothes, whether there were two of you, cameras, and your weapon. So the wrong gun on your back, a car three streets away, and a witness who saw you. That's how a GTA 5 habit ends a GTA 6 heist. Change your clothes, split up, lose the gun.

Now, the rumours. Not confirmed. But I'd love it if they're true. Passing your gun to your partner when they run out of ammo. Overhearing a stranger's conversation that leads to a new job. Golf and tennis in Vice City. And natural disasters, like a hurricane or even a tsunami hitting Leonida. None of it's confirmed by Rockstar. On Thursday we find out.
