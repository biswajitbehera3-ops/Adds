## [0:20–1:30] THE UNLOCK TIMES
*On screen: animated world map. Each country pops in with its unlock time. Use CONFIRMED stamp on the date, not on hours.*

First, the date. Rockstar has confirmed GTA 6 launches **Thursday, November 19th** on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
There's no PC version at launch.

Now, the time. Based on the store listings, the game unlocks at **midnight local time** in each country — one time zone per
country. So it rolls around the world like New Year's Eve.

**New Zealand** goes first, at midnight on the 19th — that's 6 AM Eastern on the 18th. **Australia** follows a couple of hours
later. Then Asia — **India unlocks at midnight IST**, which is about ten and a half hours before America. The **UK** unlocks at
midnight UK time, Germany and France at midnight Central European time.

And in the **United States**, it's one time for the whole country: midnight Eastern. That means if you're on the West Coast,
you can start playing at **9 PM on November 18th**.

One honest warning: Rockstar itself hasn't announced an exact launch hour. These times come from the store listings, so I'll
pin an update in the comments the moment anything changes.

## [1:30–2:45] PRE-LOAD: DO THIS TODAY
*On screen: PS5 and Xbox menus (official screenshots/press images), big "212 GB" number card with MoneyCounter-style count-up.*

If you bought the game digitally, **pre-load is open from November 10th**. On PS5 it opens at midnight your local time. On Xbox,
it opens everywhere at once, at midnight GMT.

And you really want to do this early, because the download is huge — up to **212 gigabytes**, depending on your console. That's
one of the biggest downloads ever on a console. On an average home connection, that can take many hours. If you wait until
launch night, you'll be watching a progress bar while everyone else is already playing.

So: check your free space now. If you need to, delete a couple of old games today, not on the 18th.

## [2:45–3:50] PHYSICAL COPY? READ THIS
*On screen: official box art, a big "NO DISC" stamp.*

Here's something a lot of people don't realise. The physical Standard Edition **doesn't come with a disc** — it's a download code
in a box. Physical copies reach stores from November 12th.

That means two things. One: buying the boxed version **won't save you the download**. You still need the full 212 gigabytes. Two:
be careful buying second-hand copies. A used code could already be redeemed — and then it's worthless. If you're buying it as a
gift, buy it new and sealed.

## [3:50–4:50] DON'T MISS THE FREE BONUS
*On screen: official pre-order bonus images, countdown card "NOV 20 DEADLINE".*

If you buy the game **before November 20th**, you get the **Vintage Vice City Pack** — that's a set of in-game items as a pre-order
and launch bonus. And if you pre-ordered digitally, you also get a free month of GTA+.

After the 20th, that pack is gone. So if you're on the fence, buying a day or two early actually gets you more.

## [4:50–5:40] QUICK LAUNCH-NIGHT CHECKLIST
*On screen: animated checklist, each item ticks as you say it.*

So here's your checklist before launch night:
1. **Pre-load** as soon as it opens for you.
2. **Clear 212 gigabytes** of space — today.
3. **Update your console** so it's on the latest system software.
4. If you bought a **physical copy**, redeem the code early so the download can start.
5. Buy **before the 20th** if you want the Vintage Vice City Pack.
6. Check **your country's unlock time** — it's pinned in the comments.
