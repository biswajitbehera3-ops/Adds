## THE SCRIPT

### 1. HOOK (0:00–0:20)
"You're playing GTA 6 wrong if you skip the one thing GTA 5 never had: your phone.
And that's just the first of six. GTA 5 came out thirteen years ago, and on day one of GTA 6, players found things it never had, things you'd only expect from your real phone, your real city, your real life.
Not trailer guesses. Not leaks. Only what players actually found. No story spoilers."

**[ON SCREEN:** "GTA V 2013" vs "GTA VI 2026" split card · "FOUND ON DAY ONE" stamp · "NO SPOILERS" · Trailer 1 opening shots]

### 2. THE PHONE IS A REAL PHONE (0:20–1:20)
"Number one. And it's the one everybody's posting about: the phone.
In GTA 5 your phone was basically a menu. Call a contact, open the internet, take a selfie.
In GTA 6, the top post on the GTA 6 subreddit is just someone scrolling. There's a social feed. You can open people's profiles. Read the comments under their posts. Check your texts, the news, the weather.
You can actually doom-scroll inside GTA.
And it's not just social media. There's a Reminders app, like the one on your actual phone. We saw it come up on two different launch streams.
Rockstar didn't build a phone menu this time. They built a phone."

**[ON SCREEN:** our phone mock-up, apps lighting up one by one: FEED · PROFILES · COMMENTS · TEXTS · NEWS · WEATHER · REMINDERS · a "GTA V: MENU / GTA VI: PHONE" card · official Trailer 1 social-media shots · SourceTag SEEN (player video + 2 streams)]

### 3. A GPS THAT TALKS (1:20–1:55)
"Number two. In GTA 5 you followed a purple line on the minimap and hoped.
In GTA 6, the GPS talks you through it. Turn left. Take the next right. Every turn, out loud, like Google Maps.
It sounds small. But on a map this big, with traffic this real, it changes how you drive. You actually look at the road instead of the corner of the screen."

**[ON SCREEN:** a minimap line vs a voice-wave chip "TURN LEFT IN 200 FT" · Trailer 2 highway shots · SourceTag SEEN (2 streams)]

### 4. CRIME DOESN'T PAY… YET (1:55–2:45)
"Number three changes how robberies work.
In GTA 5, you robbed a store and the cash just appeared. Instant.
In GTA 6, when you steal goods, you don't get paid on the spot. You take them to a lockup, drop them off, and the money comes later, once they've been collected.
So stealing is now a job with steps. Steal it, store it, wait, get paid.
It's a tiny change that makes the whole crime loop feel more like a real criminal operation and less like an arcade."

**[ON SCREEN:** a 4-step flow STEAL → STORE → WAIT → PAID · a "GTA V: INSTANT CASH" crossed out · a cash counter ticking up late · SourceTag SEEN (2 streams)]

### RETENTION BEAT (2:45–2:55)

### 5. BOOKS YOU CAN READ (2:55–3:30)
"Number four. On one launch stream, a player picked up a book. And opened it. And the pages were readable. Actual writing, page after page.
And the counter on screen said it was one of twenty-seven: magazines, books and comics you can collect.
In GTA 5, a book was a texture on a shelf. Here, someone at Rockstar wrote them."

### 6. THE OCEAN IS A PLACE NOW (3:30–4:05)
"Number five: water.
GTA 5 had a submarine, sure. But the ocean was mostly blue fog.
IGN has already put out a full gameplay video of scuba diving and treasure hunting in GTA 6. And reviewers keep saying the same thing about the water, and the storms, and even the hair: it looks real.
Vice City sits on the ocean. This time the ocean is part of the map, not the edge of it."

**[ON SCREEN:** official trailer ocean and beach shots · a "SCUBA · TREASURE" chip · "GTA V: EDGE OF MAP / GTA VI: PART OF IT" · SourceTag REPORTED · IGN]

### 7. WEATHER THAT CHANGES THE CITY (4:05–4:45)
"Number six: the weather.
GTA 5 had rain. It made the roads shiny and that was about it.
In GTA 6, storms roll in off the ocean. You saw it in the trailers: palm trees bending, water on the streets, the sky going dark over Vice City. And now that people are playing, reviewers keep putting the weather next to the water and the hair as the things that look real.
When a storm hits, the whole city looks like a different place. Same street, different game."

**[ON SCREEN:** official trailer storm shots · a "GTA V: SHINY ROADS / GTA VI: STORMS" card · a "STORMS" word graphic · SourceTag CONFIRMED · official trailers / REPORTED · reviews]

### 8. WHAT'S DIFFERENT IN THE OTHER DIRECTION (4:45–5:35)
"Now, to be fair, some things go the other way.
First person. GTA 5 eventually got a full first-person mode. GTA 6, at launch, doesn't have one. Rockstar's Rob Nelson reportedly said you only go first person when you aim.
Thirty frames per second on console. Reviewers liked basically everything else, but that's the one they kept naming.
And one thing GTA has had for twenty-five years is just gone. Polygon noticed there are no sex-worker NPCs in this game at all.
So it's not 'GTA 5 but bigger'. It's a different kind of game."

**[ON SCREEN:** "FIRST PERSON?" → "ONLY WHEN AIMING" stamp · amber "30 FPS" chip · a "25 YEARS → GONE" card · SourceTag REPORTED · Insider Gaming / reviews / Polygon]
