**Standard:** `production/style-guide.md` · **Runtime:** ~6:00 · **VO:** ~880 words · **Voice:** Fenrir (or the user's own voice)
**Music:** a lo-fi Miami synth bed → a darker pulse for the police section → a bright pad for the outro. No GTA VI soundtrack music.
**Spoilers:** NONE. Systems and free roam only. No missions, characters' story or cutscenes.

**Title (pick one, ≤60 chars):**
- A: `I Watched 17 Hours of GTA 6 So You Don't Have To` (48)
- B: `17 Hours of GTA 6 Streams Taught Me These 10 Things` (51)

**Thumbnail concepts:**
1. A big "$527 GONE" in red block caps over the official Trailer 2 police-chase shot, with an orange "17 HRS" clock badge in the corner. (The title linter said the thumbnail shouldn't repeat the title.)
2. The official key art of Jason and Lucia, darkened, with 6 white stars across the top, the 6th glowing red, plus "6 STARS?!".

**Why this video:** "Secrets / things you need to know" lists are the post-launch winner (T5G "30 Secrets" 3.8×, BreeZe's formula 150×). Our angle is different and honest: we actually went through a 17-hour launch stream (audio scan + a frame every minute) plus 6 other streams. No other Indian channel has done the homework version.

**Facts and tags (each item is said with its tag):**
1. **Phone: social feed, profiles, comments, news, weather, Reminders.** SEEN · r/GTA6 top post + 2 streams.
2. **Turn-by-turn spoken GPS.** SEEN · 2 streams.
3. **Stolen goods pay out later via a lockup.** SEEN · 2 streams.
4. **Red stars = police can see you; white stars = they lost you and are searching.** SEEN · frame-checked on one launch stream → say "on the stream we watched".
5. **Police tasers ("Stop or I'll hit you with 4,000 volts").** SEEN · 3 streams/videos.
6. **Getting busted cost one streamer $527.** SEEN · 1 stream → "one streamer".
7. **Store safes: the game tells you a safe code is sometimes kept at the location or known by staff.** SEEN · the game's on-screen tip, 1 stream.
8. **Sharks in the ocean.** SEEN · 4 videos (stream + 3 creators) + IGN scuba video.
9. **You can steal planes, even military jets, and fighter jets chase you.** SEEN · 5+ creators (TmarTn2, GameV, Swiss001, BreeZe, TGG guide).
10. **A 6-star wanted level exists.** REPORTED · IGN ("become a complete psychopath").

**Do NOT say:** streamers' names in VO (user rule); story or mission details; "Rockstar confirmed" for anything only seen on streams.

**Footage needed:** official trailers (1, 2, Launch, Extended Look), the official key art, the official Leonida map (Media page). Our graphics for every item. **No streamer footage.**
