## 4. Video SEO — what actually gets views

**The honest version:** YouTube recommends a video when people **click it** (thumbnail + title) and **keep watching** (retention and
satisfaction). Keywords decide *which searches and topics* you're tested in; click-through rate and watch time decide whether you
keep getting shown. Tags barely matter.

### Title (most important text)
- Put the **exact search phrase in the first ~40 characters** (phones cut the rest): "GTA 6 Wanted Level…", "GTA 6 Unlock Time…".
- Then the curiosity/benefit: "…That Will Get You Caught", "…(Every Time Zone)".
- ≤60 characters. Run it through `yt-package/title.py` with the thumbnail text.

### Phrases people search around launch (use as titles/descriptions)
| Before 19 Nov | Launch week | After launch |
|---|---|---|
| GTA 6 release time / unlock time | GTA 6 first mission | GTA 6 how to make money |
| GTA 6 pre-load size | GTA 6 tips for beginners | GTA 6 best cars / where to find |
| GTA 6 PS5 vs PS5 Pro | GTA 6 map size / all locations | GTA 6 safehouses / properties |
| GTA 6 Xbox One / cloud | GTA 6 wanted level explained | GTA 6 heists guide |
| GTA 6 physical copy / no disc | GTA 6 things to do first | GTA 6 Easter eggs / secrets |
| GTA 6 price / editions | GTA 6 vs GTA 5 | GTA 6 Online release |

(Search demand here is inferred from competitor titles and views, not measured. Check YouTube's search autocomplete and the
Studio "Research" tab once the channel exists.)

### Description (every video)
1. First 2 lines: say what the video answers, with the main phrase ("The GTA 6 wanted level has six stars and…").
2. Chapters (`0:00 …`), which become searchable "key moments" in Google.
3. Sources, linked (our trust angle).
4. 3 hashtags max at the end: `#GTA6 #GTAVI #GTA6News`. More than 15 and YouTube ignores all of them.

### Other levers
- **Captions:** upload an accurate .srt (YouTube indexes the text, so it's free keyword coverage).
- **Playlists by topic**, with keyword titles: "GTA 6 News (Daily)", "GTA 6 Guides & Tips", "GTA 6 Hidden Details",
  "GTA 6 Police & Wanted Level". Playlists rank in search and chain watch time.
- **End screen + pinned comment** pointing at the next video in the series (watch-time chains help recommendations most).
- **Shorts from every long video** (`yt-shorts`), linked to the long one with "related video". Shorts bring subscribers; long
  videos earn money.
- **Two thumbnails per video** with YouTube's Test & Compare.
- **Upload consistently** at the same times (~6 PM and ~9 PM IST, i.e. US morning/midday) so the first-hour views come from
  subscribers who are awake.
- **Stay in one niche** (GTA 6) until the channel takes off. Random topics confuse who YouTube shows it to.
- **Don't**: put fake "leaked" claims in titles (it gets clicks and then dislikes and "not interested"), keyword-stuff titles,
  reupload other creators' videos, or buy views/subs (YouTube detects it and suppresses the channel).
